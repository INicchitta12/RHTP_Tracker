Notes

1. Data date: 2026-11-11 (year1_summary_2026-11-11.csv, built by R/08 from the committed award files; completion status from year1_completion_status.csv). Year 1 = Budget Period 1 only; Year 2 is not included.

2. A state is COMPLETE only where a state source (or CMS) says Year 1 awarding is complete or Year 1 funds are fully obligated; the phrase column quotes it. 'Fully obligated' (UT, VT) is a statement that the money is committed, not that every award is published: their award lists are floors.

3. Hospital $ (best estimate) includes judgment calls on rows whose source does not settle whether a hospital receives the money (and UT's $9,891,739 Utah Hospital Association pool). Strict named-hospital $ does not: it counts only awards to a named hospital, coded from the source. Total best estimate $660,724,120; strict $624,864,060.

4. Intents are notices of intent to award, not signed agreements, and may change before execution. $227,794,537 of the best estimate (34.5%) is intents, in Arkansas, Utah, Wyoming (Wyoming's are advisory-committee approvals).

5. Awards published is the sum of published per-award amounts plus pool-level amounts the state published without a per-recipient split (Georgia $106.4M, Wyoming $267,000). It falls short of the allotment by state administration and by awards made but not itemised.

6. Figures are not comparable as hospital 'shares of need': states differ in how much of Year 1 went to hospitals by design (e.g. Utah and Florida routed most money to non-hospital and capital programmes).

7. No state was newly set COMPLETE in this build.
