Notes

1. Data date: 2026-11-13 (year1_summary_2026-11-13.csv, built by R/08 from the committed award files; completion status from year1_completion_status.csv). Year 1 = Budget Period 1 only; Year 2 is not included.

2. A state is COMPLETE only where a state source (or CMS) says Year 1 awarding is complete or Year 1 funds are fully obligated, and no Year 1 remainder is known; the phrase column quotes it. 'Fully obligated' or 'committed' (NE, SD, UT, VT, WV) says the money is committed, not that every award is published: those award lists are FLOORS, and where awards published are well below the allotment (Nebraska, South Dakota, West Virginia) the hospital % of awards describes the published awards only, not the state's whole Year 1.

3. Hospital $ (best estimate) includes judgment calls on rows whose source does not settle whether a hospital receives the money (and UT's $9,891,739 Utah Hospital Association pool). Strict named-hospital $ does not: it counts only awards to a named hospital, coded from the source. Total best estimate $755,163,077; strict $697,617,608.

4. Intents are notices of intent to award, not signed agreements, and may change before execution. $229,673,155 of the best estimate (30.4%) is intents, in Arkansas, Nebraska, Utah, Wyoming (Wyoming's are advisory-committee approvals).

5. Awards published is the sum of published per-award amounts plus pool-level amounts the state published without a per-recipient split (Georgia $106.4M, Wyoming $267,000). It falls short of the allotment by state administration and by awards made but not itemised.

6. Figures are not comparable as hospital 'shares of need': states differ in how much of Year 1 went to hospitals by design (e.g. Utah and Florida routed most money to non-hospital and capital programmes).

7. Newly COMPLETE this session: Nebraska (DHHS Year 2 release, 2026-11-10: 'fully obligated'; data/evidence/recheck/2026-11-13/NE/); South Dakota (Governor/DOH Year 2 release KB0047476, 2026-11-10: 'committed every dollar'; data/evidence/recheck/2026-11-13/SD/); West Virginia (Governor/DH Year 2 release, 2026-11-09: 'obligated 100 percent'; data/evidence/recheck/2026-11-13/WV/).
