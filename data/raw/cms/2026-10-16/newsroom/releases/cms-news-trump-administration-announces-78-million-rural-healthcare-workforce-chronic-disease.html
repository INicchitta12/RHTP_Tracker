Trump Administration Announces $78 Million for Rural Healthcare Workforce, Chronic Disease Prevention, and Facility Upgrades Across Texas

These investments will support the rural health workforce, modernize aging equipment, and advance chronic disease prevention to keep quality care within reach for rural Texans.

Today, the Trump Administration announced $78 million in Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) funding to 216 rural healthcare providers through the Rural Texas Strong initiative to strengthen healthcare across rural Texas communities. This investment will support chronic disease prevention and improved health outcomes, rural healthcare workforce recruitment and retention, and medical equipment and facility improvements to expand access to state-of-the-art care across Texas.

This transformational funding will deliver $78 million directly to 216 rural healthcare providers, helping rural communities prevent and manage chronic diseases such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, chronic respiratory disease, and obesity. Other initiatives are focused on keeping the best aspects of a small-town medical practice by recruiting and retaining doctors, emergency medical personnel, and other healthcare workers. These investments will help rural Texas communities upgrade aging medical equipment and facilities, bringing modern, reliable care closer to home for rural Texans.

The Rural Health Transformation Program is expanding access to quality healthcare, regardless of a patient’s zip code, and equipping first responders with the lifesaving resources they need when every second counts. This funding will make critical investments to strengthen rural healthcare, expand access to lifesaving services, and bring modern technology to communities across Texas.

Today’s announcement is one part of the larger overall funding amount awarded to Texas for fiscal year 2026. 

Key initiatives the overall RHTP funding will focus on include: 

  • Building the rural healthcare workforce: Supporting recruitment, training, and retention strategies to strengthen the pipeline of healthcare professionals serving rural communities. 
  • Strengthening rural facilities and access: Supporting partnerships, operational improvements, and care-delivery changes that help rural providers maintain essential services and improve access. 
  • Advancing innovative care and payment models: Supporting new approaches to delivery and financing designed to improve quality, strengthen primary care, and support sustainable rural healthcare.
  • Expanding community-based and preventive care: Investing in school-based care, mobile services, and other community strategies that improve prevention, address chronic disease, and expand access to care.
  • Modernizing rural healthcare technology: Expanding remote care and data-driven tools to improve coordination and extend healthcare services to rural communities.

The Trump Administration is delivering on its commitment to strengthen rural America by investing in solutions that modernize technology to promote health and well-being for rural communities. Led by CMS, the RHTP is moving beyond the failed one-size-fits-all approach and giving rural communities the tools to build stronger, more innovative, and more sustainable health systems that deliver better care and better outcomes for the Americans they serve.

For more information about the RHTP, visit: https://www.medicaid.gov/resources-for-states/rural-health.
