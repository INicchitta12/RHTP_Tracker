WHAT IT IS. A narrative reading of CMS's RHTP Obligated Funds Report, citing
the 10-14 edition this repository archived in session 113
(data/raw/cms/obligations/2026-07-31/RHTP-Obligated-Funds-20261014.pdf),

DEFECTS NOTED ON READING (recorded so nobody quotes them from here):
  * Its "More than 75% obligated (10 states)" bullet lists seven states;
    its "Less than 10% obligated (15 states)" bullet lists eight, one with no
    name ("(2.7%)").
  * Table B lists "Maine Rural Hospital Efficiency $30M" and "Maine EMR
    $30M" (and "Maine APM transition $28.5M") as "Illustrative Obligated
    Efforts". CMS's report has neither for Maine as of 7/31: Maine's whole
    Initiative 5 is $2,072,283.49 and its EMR line is $440,000 to the Maine
    Department of Education. Those three figures are Maine's ANNOUNCED POOLS
    (Tier 2), and session 113 found neither the RHEF nor the EMR pool awarded
    on any Maine source (data/evidence/recheck/2026-11-03/ME/).
  * It reads "obligated" as "legally committed via sub-awards, contracts, or
    agreements". CMS's own cover says the figures are state-submitted
    obligations to "state agencies, contractors and vendors, community
    partners, hospitals and facilities", which this repository treats as
    OBLIGATION BY ACTIVITY, never an award (CLAUDE.md §10, session 97).
