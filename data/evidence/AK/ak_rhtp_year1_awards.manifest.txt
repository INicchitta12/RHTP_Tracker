WHAT THIS DOCUMENT SUPPORTS (spec 9.2, Part A of reviewer-coding-instructions)

THE 161-VS-142 GAP IS RESOLVED HERE. CMS states '142 projects' for Alaska.
The file holds 161 rows because 19 of them are PLANNING awards and 142 are
IMPLEMENTATION awards -- Alaska's own Project Type column, corroborated
independently by the App ID prefix (BP1-PL appears on exactly 19). Both
figures are right and they count different things. Nothing is dropped and
no average is taken (session 11 left this open with a different and wrong
hypothesis: one line per activity type).

THESE ARE NOTICES OF INTENT TO AWARD AND THE AMOUNTS ARE PRELIMINARY.
Alaska says both in its own sheet name and its own column header. Rows carry
recipient_confirmed = Yes and amount_confirmed = No: §9.3 splits those two
questions precisely so a preliminary figure does not drag a confirmed
recipient down with it.

THIS IS A SNAPSHOT OF A ROLLING FILE. DOH is releasing notices weekly and
has said it will publish a comprehensive dashboard once all awards are
announced. notification_date is on every row; diff a later pull against this
digest rather than replacing it blind.
