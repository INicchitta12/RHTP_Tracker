Nevada Health Authority

Nevada's unified home for health care coverage, benefits, and care quality - serving 1 in 3 Nevadans.

Get Coverage

OUR PURPOSE

A New Era for Nevada Health. 

Formerly the Nevada Department of Health and Human Services, NVHA was created by Senate Bill 494 in the 2025 Legislative Session.

NVHA is Nevada's new home for PEBP, Nevada Medicaid, Nevada Health Link, and other critical programs.

OUR MISSION

Unified. Accountable. Built for Nevadans.

We work to ensure access to affordable, reliable care by leveraging the state's buying power, streamlining programs and services, and driving better quality and more innovation in the health care system, while staying true to the values of public service, fiscal discipline, and accountable leadership.

OUR PLAN

A Roadmap for a Healthier Nevada.

NVHA's 3-Year Strategic Plan charts a course to expand access, improve outcomes, and strengthen accountability across all programs.

Divisions Under NVHA

Let's get you what you need!

State Employees and Retirees

The Public Employees' Benefits Program (PEBP) administers a group health and life insurance program which offers comprehensive medical, prescription drug, dental, vision and life insurance. 

Public Employees' Benefits Program (PEBP)

This division is responsible for administering the state's Medicaid program which provides no-cost health care coverage to about one in four eligible Nevadans. 

Nevada Medicaid

This Division is responsible for overseeing the "front door" to accessing coverage programs in Nevada, including Nevada Health Link, Nevada's Market Stabalization program and Battle Born State Plans. It also works to improve health insurance literacy in Nevada.

Nevada Health Link

Nevada Health Link is Nevada’s official health insurance marketplace, offering Affordable Care Act (ACA) certified health and dental plans. Through NevadaHealthLink.com, individuals and families can compare a range of coverage options from multiple insurance companies in one place. The marketplace also provides helpful tools, free local assistance, and resources to make it easier to understand your choices and select the plan that best fits your needs and budget.

Nevada Health Link

This Division is responsible for overseeing state licensure for health facilities, Medicaid Inspector General office, compliance activities for NVHA coverage programs, and the implementation of the state's strategy for leveraging the state's buying power to get a better deal for Nevadans with respect to health care coverage. 

Bureau of Health Care Quality and Compliance 

HCQC promotes the safety and welfare of the public through regulation, licensing, enforcement, education, advocacy of quality health care and promotion of health development and protection of children.
