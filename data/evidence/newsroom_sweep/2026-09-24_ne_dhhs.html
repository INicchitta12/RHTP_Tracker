Public Records Requests​​

All public records or Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests should be sent to the DHHS Public Records Office at DHHS.PublicRecords@nebraska.gov. Per state statute, any requests for data not on the Department's public domain are to be submitted via public records request.
