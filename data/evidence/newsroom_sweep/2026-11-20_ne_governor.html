Gov. Pillen: Applications Open for Scholarship Granting Organizations in Nebraska

November 16, 2026

LINCOLN, NE – Today, Governor Jim Pillen announced that applications are now open to organizations interested in becoming approved Scholarship Granting Organizations (SGOs) in Nebraska as part of the Federal Scholarship Tax Credit (FSTC) program. The FSTC creates a new opportunity for Nebraskans to support educational opportunities in their communities through federal tax credits. The program does not reduce existing state appropriations to public schools.
