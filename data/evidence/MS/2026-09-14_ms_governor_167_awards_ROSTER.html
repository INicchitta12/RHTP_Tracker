Governor Reeves Announces 167 Rural Health Transformation Program Awards Totaling More Than $104 Million

JACKSON, Miss. – Governor Tate Reeves today announced 167 awards totaling $104,115,146 through the Mississippi Rural Health Transformation Program. The awards are part of Mississippi’s $205.9 million first-year implementation budget, which will be allocated this year. They will help modernize, strengthen and transform healthcare in rural communities across the state.

“When we began this work, we set out to do more than simply invest in the healthcare system we already have,” said Governor Tate Reeves. “We set out to transform how rural Mississippians access care – bringing more services closer to home, strengthening the providers and facilities that rural communities depend on, and using technology and innovation to better connect patients to care across our state. Today, we are beginning to see that vision become reality.”

The awards Governor Reeves announced represent only the first three grant opportunities in Year One of a five-year transformation of rural healthcare in Mississippi.

Applications for the Mississippi Rural Health Transformation Program’s workforce and psychiatric emergency services initiatives are currently being reviewed and will be announced in the next 30 to 45 days. These investments will help Mississippi train, recruit and retain the healthcare professionals rural communities need.

Additionally, in October, Mississippi will launch additional opportunities focused on innovative ways to close gaps in rural care and helping rural providers modernize the electronic health record systems they rely on every day.
