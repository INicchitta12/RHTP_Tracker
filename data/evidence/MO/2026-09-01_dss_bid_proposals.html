Bid Proposals

The Department of Social Services (DSS) solicits bids, awards contracts and initiates purchase orders in accordance with applicable statutes, policies, and guidelines. DSS promotes competitive procurement and encourages the participation and expansion of minority and women business enterprises in the procurement process. The bid table has links to bid specification files describing goods or services DSS desires to acquire. Interested parties are requested to submit bids to the location listed in the bid specification files. Specific questions should be directed to the contact point listed in each bid specification file.
