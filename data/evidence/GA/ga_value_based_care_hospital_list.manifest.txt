WHY THIS PAGE IS AN AWARD SOURCE AND NOT AN ELIGIBILITY LIST (spec 0.3)

  The page's own heading reads "Hospitals With Completed Applications", and read
  alone that is an application roster, not an award roster -- exactly the
  eligibility-is-not-receipt trap spec 0.3 exists to catch. It is not read alone.
  Both DCH award announcements link to THIS url and say what it is:

    Phase 3 (2026-07-23): "Eighty rural hospitals have completed the application
    to participate in the AHEAD model and have been awarded $750,000 to support
    pre-implementation activities. The list of 80 awarded hospitals is
    available here." -- where "here" is this url.

    Phase 4 (2026-08-27): "These awards follow the 80 rural hospital awards
    announced in Phase 3 and complete the planned group of 87 hospitals
    identified in Year 1. The full list of hospitals is available here."
    -- again this url.

  So the award action, the count and the per-hospital figure come from the DCH
  announcements; this page supplies only the names. Neither document supports
  the coding on its own, and every one of the 87 rows cites both.

  Independent closure: the table holds exactly 87 rows, which is the group DCH
  says it completed, and 80 x $750,000 = $60,000,000 is the stated Initiative 1
  pool to the dollar.

WHICH 80 OF THE 87 CARRY THE STATED $750,000 -- AN INFERENCE, FLAGGED

  DCH states $750,000 per hospital for the Phase 3 eighty and does NOT restate a
  per-hospital figure for the Phase 4 seven. The roster does not label which
  hospitals belong to which phase, so the split is inferred from list order:
  rows 1-80 are in exact alphabetical order and row 81 breaks it, leaving
  precisely 7 rows appended out of order at the end -- the shape a page updated
  in place takes when 7 names are added to an 80-name list.

  The parser derives that break rather than hardcoding it, and refuses if the
  leading alphabetical run is not exactly 80 (rhtp_ga_ahead_roster()). The seven
  appended rows carry flag_reason PHASE_ATTRIBUTION_INFERRED and no amount.

  What does NOT depend on the inference: that all 87 are awarded hospitals, and
  that the cohort total is $60,000,000 + the unstated Phase 4 share. Only the
  per-row amount attribution does.

  archive.org is not on the egress allowlist, so the July snapshot of this page
  -- which would settle the split as fact -- could not be retrieved.

DESIGNATION

  The page states that CAH (Critical Access Hospital) and RRC (Rural Referral
  Center) are CMS designations. "Rural" and "In 126 Rural/Partial Rural
  Counties" are Georgia's own classifications and are NOT CMS designations, so
  they map to rural_designation = NONE and are kept verbatim in
  rural_designation_raw (spec 8: never discard the state's raw language).
