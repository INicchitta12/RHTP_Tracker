WHAT THESE DOCUMENTS SUPPORT (spec 9.2, Part A of reviewer-coding-instructions)

  These are AGENCY_PRESS_RELEASE sources from the state agency administering the
  award (DCH). Under spec 9.2 an agency press release supports a `Yes` only for
  the recipients it NAMES. That is the whole of what has been coded from them.

  RECIPIENT-LEVEL AMOUNTS ARE NOT PUBLISHED. Every page reports a total per
  INITIATIVE and then lists the awardees inside that initiative without
  splitting the figure. Per spec 6.2 the amount is never divided, so every
  named-awardee row carries amount = NA and amount_confirmed = No -- which is
  the vocabulary's expected case ("no recipient-level figure is published, not
  that verification failed"), the same posture as DE and OK.

  The two exceptions, where the state itself states a per-hospital figure, are
  the Phase 3 and Phase 4 AHEAD pre-implementation cohorts at $750,000 each.

KNOWN GAP -- THE 87 AHEAD HOSPITAL NAMES

  Phase 3 awards 80 rural hospitals $750,000 each and Phase 4 adds 7 more, for a
  planned Year 1 group of 87. Both pages link the roster to

    https://greathealth.georgia.gov/value-based-care-hospital-list

  greathealth.georgia.gov is NOT on the egress allowlist (CONNECT rejected, 403)
  and could not be fetched in this session. dch.georgia.gov and gov.georgia.gov
  were allowlisted; this third Georgia host was not.

  The cohorts are therefore carried as two aggregate rows, not as 87 named rows.
  Nothing is imputed: the count, the per-hospital amount and the hospital
  identity of the class all come from the DCH pages archived here. Allowlist
  greathealth.georgia.gov and the roster expands to 87 named rows -- the largest
  single block of hospital-directed RHTP dollars found in any state so far.
