Virginia Medicaid Launches Cardinal Care Health Plans and Member Options 

07.01.2025

RICHMOND – Today, Governor Youngkin and the Virginia Department of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS) announced the launch of the new Cardinal Care Managed Care program. The five health plans that will serve Virginians eligible for Medicaid beginning July 1, 2025, are: Aetna Better Health of Virginia, Anthem HealthKeepers Plus, Humana Healthy Horizons of Virginia (new), Sentara Health Plans, and UnitedHealthcare of the Mid-Atlantic, Inc. The program also includes a foster care specialty plan administered by Anthem HealthKeepers Plus. This initiative aligns with Governor Youngkin’s goal of ensuring that Virginia’s vulnerable populations receive high-quality health care coverage.

Virginia Medicaid Announces Intent to Award Managed Care Contracts

02.29.2024

Richmond – The Department of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS) posted a Notice of Intent to Award (NOIA) contracts after completing evaluations and negotiations of the proposals received in response to RFP 13330, Cardinal Care Managed Care (CCMC) program. DMAS intends to award statewide contracts for the CCMC program to Anthem HealthKeepers Inc., Aetna Better Health of Virginia, Humana Healthy Horizons of Virginia, Sentara Health Plans, and United Healthcare of the Mid-Atlantic, Inc.

Virginia Medicaid to Provide 12 Months of Protected Coverage for Children

01.10.2024

Richmond – The Virginia Department of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS) announced that children below the age of 19 enrolled in Virginia’s Medicaid and Family Access to Medical Insurance Security (FAMIS) programs will receive a 12-month period of protected coverage effective January 1, 2024. DMAS currently serves more than 800,000 Virginian children.
