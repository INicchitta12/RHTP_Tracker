Rural Health Transformation/Ways to Apply

Requests for Applications for Virginia’s Rural Health Transformation (RHT) funding will open over the coming weeks, offering organizations across the Commonwealth a significant opportunity to advance innovative, community-driven solutions that improve access to care, strengthen the healthcare workforce, and support long-term sustainability in rural communities.

Additional funding opportunities will be released on a rolling basis throughout the Summer and early Fall, providing multiple entry points for partners to engage and align their initiatives with statewide priorities.

Use the Request for Applications RHT RFA Navigator to see when RFAs open, explore key focus areas, and learn who to contact for additional information specific to each RFA
