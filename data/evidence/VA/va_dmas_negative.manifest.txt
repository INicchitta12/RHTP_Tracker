WHY DMAS IS NOT WHERE THE AWARDS WILL POST -- A CORRECTION TO SESSION 14

Session 14 reasoned that "DMAS runs the RFA-RHTP-2026-nn series and is where
the awards will post". The first half is right and the second is wrong, and
the agency's own procurement page says so: DMAS hosts no solicitations of its
own. /about-us/procurement/ directs vendors to eVA, the Commonwealth's central
procurement portal, for every DMAS opportunity. The RFA application portals
named in the committed RCJ pull are third-party pass-through administrators --
vhcf.org (Virginia Health Care Foundation), vhha.com / vhhafoundation.org
(the Virginia Hospital & Healthcare Association Foundation) -- and the
programme's own site is ruralhealthtransformationva.virginia.gov.

ALL of those hosts are REFUSED at CONNECT from this session, as are
eva.virginia.gov, vdh.virginia.gov and hhr.virginia.gov. Allowlisting
dmas.virginia.gov answered the question asked of it; it did not open the
route to Virginia's awards, because that route does not run through DMAS.
