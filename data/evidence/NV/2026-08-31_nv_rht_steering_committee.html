Nevada is embarking on a statewide effort to transform our rural healthcare system through the Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program. Working hand-in-hand with local communities, Tribal nations, and regional partners, Nevada intends to strengthen primary care, chronic disease management, support  maternal and child health, and expand behavioral health services in rural areas.

To guide implementation of this important work, Nevada will establish a Rural Health Transformation Steering Committee (RHTSC) early in 2026. Members will be appointed by the Nevada Health Authority Director with approval from the Governor. The Committee will monitor progress across all initiatives and recommend adjustments to ensure the Rural Health Transformation Plan maintains alignment with federal goals and delivers lasting, transformational improvements in rural healthcare statewide. The RHTSC will be composed of a broad array of representatives from rural health systems and communities in addition to state, local, and tribal health representatives.

RHTSC Meetings are held quarterly and are subject to Nevada's Open Meeting Law. The next meeting is scheduled for June 9, 2026. Please visit RHT Meetings for the most current information, including details from prior meetings.

The State is committed to supporting transparent, accountable, and community-driven strategies through the RHT Program. To ensure meaningful public involvement, the RHTSC will convene quarterly, with each meeting open to the public and featuring two dedicated public comment periods. This structure is designed to promote transparency, encourage community input, and foster ongoing stakeholder engagement. The RHTSC will serve as the primary governance and stakeholder engagement body for rural healthcare in Nevada, with representation from the following groups:

  • Director or his or her designee of the Nevada Health Authority (NVHA)
  • Director or his or her designee of the Governor’s Office of Economic Development (GOED)
  • Administrator or his or her designee of the Division of Public and Behavioral Health, Nevada Department of Human Services (DPBH)
  • Tribal Representative pending establishment of the Nevada Tribal Health Authority (NTHA)
  • Representative from the Nevada Rural Hospital Partners (NRHP)
  • Representative from the Nevada Primary Care Association (NVPCA)
  • Representative from the Nevada State Office of Rural Health (SORH)
  • Representative from the Nevada Higher System of Education (NSHE)
  • Representative from Great Basin College (GBC)
  • Rural or frontier county official from the Nevada Association of Counties (NACO)
  • A frontier county consumer representative
  • A rural county consumer representative
  • Representative from a rural Certified Community Behavioral Health Center
  • Representative from a rural emergency medical services (EMS) provider
  • Representative with expertise in population health management
