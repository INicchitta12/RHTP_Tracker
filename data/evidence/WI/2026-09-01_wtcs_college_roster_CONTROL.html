College Contacts

The WTCS has 16 technical college districts with 47 campuses throughout the state. Each WTCS district has a cadre of highly qualified, certified fire service instructors. All 16 districts offer approved fire service training courses.

The fire service contacts at the technical college districts can assist you in scheduling training courses or certification testing, and enrolling in fire service or hazardous materials related associate degree and/or technical diploma programs.
