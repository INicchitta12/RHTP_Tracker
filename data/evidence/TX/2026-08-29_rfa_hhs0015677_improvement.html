Program Name: Financial Services

Executive Summary

The Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC), the System Agency, is accepting applications for the Rural Hospital Improvement Grant.


The purpose of this program is to provide time-limited financial support during the term of the Grant Agreement for any qualified Rural Hospital in Texas to support hospital improvement activities aimed to improve the financial stability of the hospital.


Applicants should reference Section II, Scope of Grant Project, for further detailed information regarding the purpose, background, eligible population, eligible activities and requirements.
