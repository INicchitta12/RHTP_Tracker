Rural Hospital Financial Assistance

The Financial Assistance team, also known as the Grants team, in the Office of Rural Hospital Finance (RHF), administers and oversees rural hospital grants. The Financial Assistance team serves as the primary point of contact for the administration and management of state-funded grants to rural hospitals. The team helps stakeholders with questions about grant payments, funding availability, reporting requirements, and the status of financial support programs for rural hospitals.

Rural Grant Library

RHF has created a Rural Grant Library that includes rural state, federal, foundation and corporate grants. The Rural Grant Library’s purpose is to have a common resource for rural hospitals to research open and upcoming grants. Please note, some grants that are identified as “closed” in the library remain listed because they may be recurring grants (depending on availability of state and federal funding); and knowing when their annual application periods open and close is helpful for planning purposes.

The Excel file provides a summary of key information about each grant opportunity. Users can filter the following information to locate grants by: Anticipated Award Amount, Targeted Provider, Status, Application Deadline, Funding Focus Area, Opportunity Name & Number, Funding Agency, and Additional Information. To learn the details of each grant, click on the “Opportunity Name & Number” of each grant.

89th Texas Legislature, Regular Session, 2025

The 89th Texas Legislature, Regular Session, 2025, enacted the Rural Health Stabilization and Innovation Act (House Bill 18). Among other important functions, it established multiple grant programs for rural hospitals aimed at improving their financial stability, fostering innovation, expanding pediatric tele-connectivity services, and addressing emergency hardship events.

Open Grant Opportunities

Pediatric Tele-Connectivity Resource Program for Rural Texas

HHSC is now accepting applications for the fiscal year 2026 Pediatric Tele-Connectivity Resource Program. The grant provides funding to rural hospitals and certain rural health clinics to connect the hospital or clinic with pediatric specialists through telemedicine services or to obtain consultations with specialists at institutions of higher education. Eligible clinics need to be located in a county that does not contain a licensed general hospital or special hospital.

HHSC will award up to $10 million in grants for fiscal year 2026. Awards will range from $1 million to $2 million.

Applications must be submitted using the Grants Management System (GMS) External Portal and are due July 13 at 10:30 AM (CDT).

Closed Grant Opportunities

Financial Stabilization Grant Program

The fiscal year 2026-2027 Financial Stabilization (FS) Grant Program provides time-limited funding to support and improve the financial stability of rural hospitals, rural hospital districts, and rural hospital authorities that are determined to be at moderate or high risk of financial instability.

To qualify for the FS Grant, a rural hospital must be identified by HHSC to be at a moderate or high risk of instability. Each awarded hospital will receive three payments over the duration of the grant period. View the Financial Stabilization Grant Details (PDF) for a list of qualified rural hospitals and anticipated award amounts.

Applications for the FS Grant closed on Feb. 5, 2026.

Rural Hospital Innovation Grant Program

The fiscal year 2026 Rural Hospital Innovation Grant program provides funding to rural hospitals, rural hospital districts, and rural hospital authorities for new initiatives that increase the availability and quality of health care for rural residents, strengthen financial stability, and are sustainable without further state funds. Proposed initiatives that focus on improving healthcare facilities or services for these populations will be prioritized:

  • pregnant women or new mothers that have recently given birth,
  • children and young adults under aged twenty,
  • older adults living in rural counties, and
  • persons who are uninsured.

HHSC will award up to $25 million in grants for fiscal year 2026. Awards will range from $2 million to $5 million.

Applications for the Rural Hospital Innovation Grant closed on May 28, 2026.

88th Texas Legislature, Regular Session, 2023

Closed HHSC Grant Opportunities

The 88th Texas Legislature appropriated $50 million to HHSC for the 2024-2025 biennium to establish grant programs for rural hospitals. The legislature identified these funds could be used for: financial stabilization, maternal care, and alternative payment model-readiness. House Bill 1 enacted in the 88th Legislature, Regular Session (2023), Article II Rider 88 appropriated the grant funding.

RHF distributed the following grants in fiscal year 2024 and 2025.

Rural Hospital Debt Reduction Grant

The Rural Hospital Debt Reduction Grant provides one-time funding to qualified rural hospitals to reduce their debt to improve the financial stability of the hospital. Each awarded hospital received a lump sum payment of $250,000.

Applications for the Rural Hospital Debt Reduction Grant closed on April 24, 2025. RHF awarded 21 grants (PDF).

Rural Hospital Debt Reduction Grant Key Reporting Dates

*Original due dates falling on weekends will be due the following Monday.

Rural Hospital Improvement Grant

The Rural Hospital Improvement Grant provides one-time funding to qualified rural hospitals to support hospital improvement activities that will contribute to the financial stability of the hospital. Each awarded hospital received a lump sum payment of $350,000.

Applications for the Rural Hospital Improvement Grant closed on April 9, 2025. RHF awarded 33 grants (PDF).

Rural Hospital Improvement Grant Key Reporting Dates

*Original due dates falling on weekends will be due the following Monday.

Rural Hospital Labor and Delivery Grant

The Rural Hospital Labor and Delivery Grant provides one-time funding to qualified rural hospitals to invest in activities that will sustain inpatient labor and delivery services. Each awarded hospital received a lump sum payment of $250,000.

Applications for the Rural Hospital Labor and Delivery Grant closed on April 8, 2025. RHF awarded 30 grants.

Rural Hospital Labor and Delivery Grant Key Reporting Dates

Funding Utilization Report and Supporting Documentation (Interim)

  • If your Grant Agreement became effective in July 2025: Due date is 2/2/2026.
  • If your Grant Agreement became effective in August 2025: Due date is 3/2/2026.

Funding Utilization Report and Supporting Documentation (Final)

  • If your Grant Agreement ends on 8/31/2026: Due Date is 8/17/2026.

Maternal Care Operations Grant

The Maternal Care Operations Grant provided one-time funding to improve rural maternal care in facilities where emergency labor and delivery services can occur. Each awarded hospital received a $35,000 lump sum payment for the purchase of neonatal equipment, supplies and training for emergency labor and delivery health care services. Rural hospitals eligible for the grant must not provide inpatient labor and delivery services.

Applications for the Maternal Care Operations Grant closed on Sept. 13, 2024. RHF awarded 59 grants.

Rural Hospital Financial Stabilization Grant

The Rural Hospital Financial Stabilization Grant provided time-limited financial support for operational costs for certain rural hospitals to assist them with improving their long-term financial solvency and sustainability. Facilities received a two-year award based on their financial need:

  1. Tier 1 (Basic Need) – Year 1 - $100,000; Year 2 - $50,000
  2. Tier 2 (Moderate Need) – Year 1 - $175,000; Year 2 - $87,500
  3. Tier 3 (High Need) – Year 1 - $250,000; Year 2 - $125,000

Applications for the Rural Hospital Financial Stabilization Grant closed on July 8, 2024.

Texas Rural Emergency Hospitals Financial Stabilization Grant

The Texas Rural Emergency Hospitals Financial Stabilization Grant Program provided time-limited grants in October 2023 to facilities that had converted to a rural emergency hospital. The grant provided financial support for hospitals transitioning into this new role as rural emergency hospitals and to support the facility’s long-term financial solvency and sustainability.

Rural hospitals that obtained both the state Limited Services Rural Hospital (LSRH) license under Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 241, Subchapter K, and the federal rural emergency hospital designation from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services by Aug. 31, 2023, were eligible to apply.

Each eligible hospital received a two-year grant totaling $1,125,000 (Year 1 amount - $750,000 and Year 2 amount - $375.000).

The Texas Rural Emergency Hospitals Financial Stabilization Grant application closed on Oct. 13, 2023. HHSC awarded four grants.
