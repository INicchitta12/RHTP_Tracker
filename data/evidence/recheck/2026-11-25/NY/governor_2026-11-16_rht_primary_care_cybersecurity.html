Governor Hochul Announces New Rural Health Transformation Initiatives to Expand Primary Care and Strengthen Cybersecurity

$30 Million Initiative Will Help Rural Primary Care Practices Expand Technology-Enabled, Coordinated Care Through the Patient-Centered Medical Home Model

$79 Million Investment Will Expand Telehealth and eConsults and Strengthen Cybersecurity Across Rural Health Care

Initiatives Are Part of New York’s More Than $212 Million First-Year Rural Health Transformation Program Award

Traducción al español

Governor Kathy Hochul today announced new investments under New York State’s Rural Health Transformation Program to expand technology-enhanced primary care and strengthen technology and cybersecurity infrastructure for rural health care providers.

The investments are part of more than $212 million New York received for the first year of the federal Rural Health Transformation Program, a $50 billion initiative administered by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services and running from federal fiscal year 2026 through 2030.

“Every New Yorker deserves access to high-quality health care, no matter where they live,” Governor Hochul said. “These investments will help rural providers deliver more coordinated care close to home, use technology to connect patients with specialists and services and strengthen critical health care systems against cyber threats.”

State Health Commissioner Dr. James McDonald said, “Rural providers face unique challenges, including workforce shortages, geographic barriers and limited resources to make major investments in technology and infrastructure. By strengthening primary care, expanding access to technology and improving cybersecurity resilience, we are helping rural health care providers build stronger, more sustainable systems of care.”

Expanding Technology-Enhanced Primary Care

New York is investing $30 million through the Strengthening Rural Communities with Technology-Enhanced Primary Care initiative to improve rural patients’ access to and utilization of high-quality primary care.

The initiative will help rural primary-care practices adopt and strengthen the Patient-Centered Medical Home model, incorporating artificial intelligence and other technology to support care delivery, quality and operational efficiency.

A primary-care practice designated as a New York State Patient-Centered Medical Home by the National Committee for Quality Assurance focuses on team-based care, patient-centered access and continuity of care, care management, coordination among providers and continuous quality improvement.

Under this model, a patient’s primary-care team serves as a central hub for coordinating care across specialists, hospitals, behavioral health providers and other services. Technology can help rural providers identify patient needs, coordinate care and connect patients with services that may not be readily available locally.

Expanding Technology and Strengthening Cybersecurity

New York is also investing over $79 million through the Investments in Technology Innovation and Cybersecurity Enhancements for Rural New York initiative.

The initiative will:

  • Expand access to health care through telehealth;
  • Improve patient outcomes through eConsult partnerships that connect providers with specialty expertise;
  • Increase usable health information alerts in rural counties; and
  • Strengthen cybersecurity protections for rural health care facilities.

These investments will help rural providers use technology to overcome geographic barriers to care while strengthening the infrastructure needed to safely deliver health care.

Specialized Partners will help put investments into practice; The New York State Department of Health is engaging three organizations with specialized expertise to support implementation of the Rural Health Transformation Program and work directly with participating rural health care providers.

Primary Care Development Corporation (PCDC) will support the Strengthening Rural Communities with Technology-Enhanced Primary Care initiative, working with rural primary-care practices to achieve the New York State Patient-Centered Medical Home model recognition and support practices participating in the model. This work will include helping practices improve team-based care, patient access, care management and coordination, quality improvement and the use of technology and data to support more efficient and sustainable primary care. Funding is also available to help rural practices make site updates that will help members with disabilities access care.

Public Consulting Group (PCG) will provide program implementation, coordination and stakeholder engagement support, helping the Department work across rural health care providers, community organizations, program participants and other partners. PCG will support coordinated implementation of Rural Health Transformation activities, outreach and engagement, and efforts to ensure providers have the information and support needed to participate successfully in the program.

Accenture LLP will support efforts to strengthen cybersecurity resilience among eligible rural health facilities across New York’s 47 rural counties. The cybersecurity resilience initiative will provide cybersecurity assessments, access to cybersecurity training, incident-response support, threat intelligence, shared regional services and targeted improvements to help protect sensitive information, reduce operational risks and maintain reliable health care services for rural communities.

Together, the implementation partners will provide technical expertise and hands-on assistance to help rural providers turn New York’s Rural Health Transformation investments into sustainable improvements in care delivery, technology, coordination and access.

Part of New York’s Broader Rural Health Transformation Strategy

The initiatives are part of New York’s broader first-year Rural Health Transformation strategy, supported by more than $212 million in federal funding.

In September, New York announced more than $76 million through the Rural Community Health Integration Initiative, supporting 90 awards to 56 organizations to strengthen regional partnerships, improve coordination of care and expand access to health and social services in rural communities.

New York’s first-year Rural Health Transformation Program includes four major initiatives:

Rural Community Health Integration Initiative — $77.2 million:Supports coordinated rural health partnerships that improve care coordination, expand access to health and social services and help rural hospitals and community providers build or grow integrated health networks.

Strengthening Rural Communities with Technology-Enhanced Primary Care — $30 million: Improves rural patient access to and utilization of high-quality primary care through the Patient-Centered Medical Home model, incorporating artificial intelligence and other technologies to improve care delivery, quality and sustainability.

Rural Roots, Building a Sustainable Rural Healthcare Workforce — $13 million: Supports recruitment, training and retention strategies to address rural health care workforce needs.

Investments in Technology Innovation and Cybersecurity Enhancements for Rural New York — $79.5 million: Expands access to care through telehealth, improves patient outcomes through eConsult partnerships, increases usable alerts in rural counties and strengthens cybersecurity at rural health care facilities.

Funding supports the four major initiatives, as well as management consulting support, staffing and travel needed to implement and administer the program.

Participation in the Rural Health Transformation Program and successful implementation of New York’s first-year investments will support the State’s ability to pursue additional federal funding in future years of the five-year program.

This project is supported by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services as part of a financial assistance award totaling more than $419,808,278.74 for Budget Periods 1 and 2, with 100 percent funded by CMS/HHS.
