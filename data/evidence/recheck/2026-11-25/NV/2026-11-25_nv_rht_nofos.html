Nevada's RHT Program Notice of Award (NOA)

 

 

The Nevada Health Authority (NVHA) is currently accepting applications for select initiatives and preparing to release additional funding opportunities under the Nevada Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) for Budget Period 2 (BP2).

These funding opportunities support Nevada’s statewide effort to improve rural health access, strengthen healthcare infrastructure, expand workforce capacity, and implement innovative care delivery models.

Prospective applicants are encouraged to begin preparing now by reviewing the Rural Health Transformation Applicant Guide – Budget Period 2, which outlines upcoming funding opportunities, key eligibility and administrative requirements, federal grant compliance expectations, and required registrations. Organizations should review these requirements and take necessary steps well in advance of applying to ensure they are prepared to meet application and award requirements.

The Rural Health Transformation Program is supported through a federal cooperative agreement authorized under Public Law 119-21, Section 71401, and administered in partnership with the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

Applicants should carefully review each Request for Application (RFA) for initiative-specific eligibility criteria, allowable uses of funds, application deadlines, and submission instructions.

Requests for Applications

For current information about the Children's Behavioral Health Transformation (CBHT) funding opportunity, eligibility requirements, application materials, and submission instructions, visit the CBHT Rural Health Transformation Grant page.

This funding opportunity supports the recruitment and retention of children's behavioral health providers in rural Nevada through the Workforce Recruitment and Rural Access Program (WRRAP).

Applications due December 10, 2026.

For current information about Nevada’s RHT funding opportunities, eligibility, and application materials, visit the Medicaid Reentry Rural Funding Opportunities page.

Applications due December 15, 2026.
