Application & Award

Rural Health Transformation/Application & Award

Please note: Applications to apply are now open and can be found in the Ways to Apply tab

The RHT Program is administered by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

  • Funding will be distributed annually from Federal Fiscal Year 2026 through 2030
  • Continued funding is contingent on a yearly federal performance and re-scoring process

To participate, states were required to submit applications in response to the federal Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) by November 5, 2025.

  • Virginia successfully submitted its application and received a Notice of Award in December 2025, securing $189.5 million in first-year funding

RHTP Budget Timeline

  • Virginia was awarded $189.5M for budget period one under RHTP
  • There are five budget periods, recipients will have until the end of the following fiscal year (September 30) to spend awarded funding
  • Funds that a State does not spend by the end of the following fiscal year, or that are not allocated by CMS will be redistributed in the upcoming fiscal year
