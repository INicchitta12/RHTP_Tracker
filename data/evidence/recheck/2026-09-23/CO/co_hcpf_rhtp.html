Colorado Rural Health Transformation Program

The Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) was authorized by H.R. 1 Section 71401 of Public Law 119-21, to provide grants totaling $50 billion in federal funds from Federal Fiscal Year (FFY) 2026 through FFY 2030 ($10 billion per year) to States (territories and Washington D.C. are excluded). The RHTP is a five-year nationwide initiative administered by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) and designed to strengthen access, improve outcomes, expand the workforce, accelerate innovative care models, and modernize technology-enabled services for rural and frontier residents. Colorado was awarded $200,105,604.17 in budget year one.

The RHTP team anticipates making our award announcements by the end of September 2026.

How to request applicant technical assistance:

HCPF has awarded a contract to the Colorado Rural Health Center to provide training and technical assistance to organizations applying for Rural Health Transformation Project (RHTP) grant funding. Interested applicants may seek training and technical assistance from the Colorado Rural Health Center by contacting the organization at CRHC_RHTP@coruralhealth.org.
