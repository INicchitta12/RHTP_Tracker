The RHTP Advisory Committee will serve as an essential advisory body in the development, implementation and governance of the RHTP, leveraging over $1 billion in federal grants during the grant period (2026–2031). As part of this work, the Committee will provide strategic direction and guidance for the entire RHTP within the guidelines of this federal grant program, ensure rural stakeholder representation, advise on funding distribution priorities and grant application development, review statewide program progress and performance data, and coordinate with technical workgroups. The Committee will also provide input on the state application process, the Request for Application and scoring criteria, and will have the opportunity to review public-facing RHTP materials prior to publication.

While the Committee will provide recommendations on projects to fund, final grant authority is outlined in the Cooperative Agreement between Health Care Policy and Financing (HCPF) and the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). HCPF also holds responsibility for compliance with all federal and state requirements and CMS grant guidelines.
