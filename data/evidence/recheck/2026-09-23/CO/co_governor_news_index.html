  • Following Federal Approval of Colorado’s Major Disaster Declaration, FEMA Denies Individual Assistance and the Hazard Mitigation Grant Funding Portions of Original Request

    Friday, September 18, 2026
    DENVER — Today, following the recent federal approval of Colorado’s Major Disaster Declaration, FEMA denied the Individual Assistance (IA) and the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP) portions of Colorado’s Major Disaster Declaration request. Individual Assistance would have provided direct assistance to individuals and families in Pueblo and Custer Counties, including for temporary housing assistance and to help rebuild homes. The Hazard Mitigation Grant funding assists resilience for communities who are rebuilding, and denying these funds could leave communities more exposed to future events.
