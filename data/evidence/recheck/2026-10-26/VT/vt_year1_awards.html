RHT Year 1 Awards and Contracts

Updated as of October 23, 2026

The list below includes executed agreements for Vermont’s Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program as of October 23, 2026. The list will be updated regularly as additional agreements are finalized and executed. A description of Vermont’s RHT activities can be found here: https://healthcarereform.vermont.gov/document/rhtp-application-activity-details-12-2025

Please note: This is a partial and ongoing list and does not represent the full Year 1 awards or funding decisions. Agreements are being finalized on an ongoing basis, and procurement and award processes remain underway for some activities. As a result, recently executed agreements may not yet appear on this list.

If an organization or agreement is not included on this list, this should not be interpreted as an indication that the organization was not selected for an RHT award. Award and funding decisions will be communicated through the applicable award or procurement process.
