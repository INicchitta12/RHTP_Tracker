Hospitals, Clinically Integrated Networks and Rural Health Innovation Hubs, and Maternal Care

October 16, 2026 | Agency

The hospitals, clinically integrated networks and rural health innovation hubs, and maternal care portions of RHT aim to improve the quality, efficiency, and coordination of patient care among rural hospitals and healthcare providers, ensuring rural Ohioans receive the right care, in the right place, at the right time, improving the overall health of the community.   

The project ensures providers have the infrastructure they need to operate as part of a robust network. Providers and entities involved include physicians, hospitals, clinics, and emergency medical services (EMS), behavioral health, pharmacists, higher education, community resources, and others. It supports clinical professionals and paraprofessionals to operate at the highest level of their knowledge and credentials by helping health systems coordinate care, standardize guidelines, foster smoother transitions, and focus on value for the patient.

  • Wilson Memorial Hospital.
  • Marion General Hospital.
  • O'Bleness Memorial Hospital.
  • Sojourn at Seneca.
  • Fisher-Titus Medical Center.
  • Mary Rutan Hospital.
  • Blanchard Valley Regional Health Center.

Clinically Integrated Networks and Rural Health Innovation Hubs

Clinically integrated networks and rural health innovation hubs are structured alliances that collaborate to improve quality, efficiency, and coordination of patient care. This project provides $31.4 million to establish/expand these structured alliances.

  • Ohio High Value Network. 
  • Child & Family Health Collaborative of Ohio.
  • Ohio State Health Network.
  • University Hospitals Health System.
  • Beavercreek Medical Center (Soin/Kettering Health).
  • Ohio Association of Community Health Centers.
  • Mercy Health - St. Rita's Medical Center (Lima).

Maternal Care

This investment dedicates $30.3 million to improving maternal care.

  • Ohio High Value Network. 
  • Ohio State Health Network.
  • Beavercreek Medical Center (Soin/Kettering Health).
  • Ohio Association of Community Health Centers.
  • Ohio Health Institute.
  • Mercy Health - St. Rita's Medical Center (Lima).
