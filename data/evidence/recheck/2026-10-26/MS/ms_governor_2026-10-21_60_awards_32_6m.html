Governor Reeves Announces 60 Rural Health Transformation Program Awards Totaling $32.6 Million

JACKSON, Miss. – Governor Tate Reeves today announced 60 awards totaling $32,606,496.32 through the Mississippi Rural Health Transformation Program. The awards are part of Mississippi’s $205.9 million first-year implementation budget and follow previously announced first-year awards of more than $104.1 million. These awards will help modernize, strengthen and transform healthcare in rural communities across the state.

“This is another really important round of awards,” said Governor Tate Reeves. “The investments we’re making today will help Mississippi train, recruit and retain the health care professionals our rural communities need and help close gaps in mental health care by strengthening our state’s Psychiatric Emergency Services. We set out to transform health care across our state, and that’s what we’re doing. These investments will help Mississippi families get the care they deserve closer to home and strengthen health care for generations to come. Thank you to President Trump, Secretary Kennedy and Administrator Oz for their partnership in improving health care for Mississippi and her people. It’s another great day for Mississippi.”

“The Trump Administration’s Rural Health Transformation Program is helping states like Mississippi develop innovative, locally driven solutions to improve rural Americans’ access to high-quality care,” said CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz. “These grant awards demonstrate how this historic investment is empowering rural communities to strengthen their health systems, put patients first, and bring better care closer to home.”

The awards Governor Reeves announced today represent the continuation of Mississippi’s work in Year One of a five-year transformation of rural healthcare in Mississippi.

In October, Mississippi is launching additional funding opportunities focused on innovative ways to close gaps in rural care and assisting small rural providers in modernizing the electronic health record systems they rely on every day. Monitor https://mississippirhtp.com for information on webinars and application opportunities.
