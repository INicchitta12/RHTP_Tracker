California Rural Health Transformation (CalRHT)

CalRHT Program Updates

Recommended Subrecipients for CalRHT Budget Period 1 Announced#

October 21, 2026

California is taking an important step toward transforming rural healthcare by distributing $166.72 million in California Rural Health Transformation Program funding for 108 organizations across 45 counties. 

These investments will help rural communities expand primary, maternity, specialty, and whole person care while strengthening the providers and facilities on which rural Californians depend.

Recommended investments are organized across three CalRHT initiatives.#
Transformative Care Model#

Approximately $87.51 million supporting 34 recommended grants, along with milestone-based hospital investments and clinical training partnerships, to expand primary, maternity, specialty, and whole person care closer to home.

Workforce Development#

Approximately $67.56 million supporting 65 recommended grants to strengthen recruitment and retention, develop rural career pathways and expand clinical training opportunities. 

Technology and Tools#

Approximately $11.65 million supporting 9 recommended grants to help rural, remote, and Tribal providers modernize electronic health records, strengthen cybersecurity, and improve patient data exchange.

Tribal Set-Aside#

CalRHT also includes dedicated funding to support California’s Tribal communities and rural Tribal healthcare delivery system. 

Five percent of California’s first-year CalRHT funding, totaling $11.65 million included within the initiative funding described above, is reserved for California’s federally recognized Tribes and the rural Tribal healthcare delivery system. The allocation was developed in consultation with Tribal leaders.

This support aims to expand access to care, improve the quality and coordination of services and help Tribal communities build stronger and more sustainable local health systems.

Contract and Program Support#

In addition to these recommended grant investments, Budget Period 1 funding will support the infrastructure and assistance needed to administer CalRHT and help participating organizations implement their work. 

CalRHT includes $56.51 million in contract support, including funding the Rural Technical Assistance Center (RTAC), and $10.41 million in HCAI program support. 

Together, these recommended investments will help expand access to care, strengthen the rural healthcare workforce, and modernize healthcare technology in rural communities across California.

Geographic Reach#

See how recommended CalRHT Program grants would reach communities across California. Explore the map to view where each type of grant would be awarded and the counties served by recommended subrecipients.

 

Note: One applicant that was recommended to receive an award has subsequently withdrawn their application.

Recommended Subrecipients for CalRHT Budget Period 1#

The organizations recommended for Budget Period 1 funding are listed below alphabetically by initiative and grant. The list reflects awards announced to date and does not yet represent all Budget Period 1 investments included in the initiative totals above. Additional recommendations will be added as they are ready for publication.

Funding amounts shown reflect the amounts offered to potential subrecipients and may differ from the final amounts disbursed. This information will be updated weekly as subrecipient agreements are executed.

All Budget Period 1 funding recommendations are subject to completion of the grant agreement and contracting process. Inclusion on this list does not constitute a final grant award.

Transformative Care Model#

#

The Accelerator Partner Program is proposed as support for hospitals or other organizations in rural regions that demonstrate strong readiness and commitment to implement the CalRHT Transformative Care Model (TCM) through regional care collaboratives and hub-and-spoke relationships which will seek to strengthen primary and maternity care in rural parts of the state. 

#

The Family Medicine Obstetrics (FM-OB) Fellowship Subaward Program under Expand and Support Rural Workforce Capacity is intended to fund the programmatic and infrastructure costs of expanding FM-OB fellowship capacity. 

#

Transformative Payments are investments in a select group of rural hospitals whose stabilization is essential to increasing rural access in California. These payments are conditional, milestone-based investments to support hospital participation in feasible components of the TCM to advance long-term sustainability, improve local utilization, and optimize rural and frontier health outcomes.

Workforce Development#

#

The Workforce Development Recruitment and Retention Program (WDRR) aims to strengthen and sustain the rural healthcare workforce by improving recruitment, retention, and training for health professionals, with a focus on those who provide primary care and maternal health services.

Technology & Tools#

#

Electronic Health Record (EHR) Modernization Grants fund the foundational systems required for basic data exchange by ensuring rural facilities can purchase and sustain modern, secure EHRs, and funding is directed to the entities and activities that support implementation and scale.

Important Award Information#

The awards listed on this page are tentative. Additional awards are pending and will be added to this page as they become available.

Funding will not be finalized until HCAI and the selected organization have fully executed a subrecipient agreement. Selected organizations should not hire staff, begin project activities, obligate funds, or incur project-related expenses before the agreement is fully executed. Costs incurred before agreement execution are not eligible for reimbursement. 

HCAI may modify or withdraw a tentative award before execution of the subrecipient agreement if required because of federal funding decisions, changes in funding availability, federal requirements, or the results of the compliance review process.

 

The CalRHT program is supported by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) as part of a financial assistance award totaling $233,639,308.46 with 100 percent funded by CMS/HHS. The contents are those of CalRHT and do not necessarily represent the official views of, nor an endorsement, by CMS/HHS, or the U.S. Government.
