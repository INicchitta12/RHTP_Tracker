Rural Health Transformation Program

What is Rural Health Transformation Program? 

  • A Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Service (CMS) grant designed to support rural communities transform the healthcare delivery ecosystem​
  • $189 Million Dollars (per year) awarded to the State of Hawai’i for five years​
  • Divided into 6 initiatives​:
    1. Rural Health Information Network (RHIN)
    2. Rural Value Based Innovation (RVBI)
    3. Rural Infrastructure for Care Access (RICA)
    4. Home Run
    5. Rural Respite Network (RRN)
    6. Pili Ola Network

Link to Hawaiʻi’s Rural Health Transformation Plan – Engage Hawai’i

What will SHPDA lead?

Initiative 1, RHIN​

  • The RHIN initiative is a competitive fund that will help rural health providers strengthen network connectivity and modernize clinical systems so they can participate successfully in the AHEAD model. Funds may support last-mile infrastructure, EHR optimization and interoperability upgrades, data exchange readiness, and technical assistance for implementation and training. Eligible applicants may include rural hospitals, independent provider practices, and Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs).​

Initiative 6, RVBI ​

  • This competitive fund will enable rural providers to adopt care models and succeed under the AHEAD model by financing local value-based innovations. This may include support for quality reporting, advanced analytics, practice transformation coaching, hiring staff, etc. This includes hospitals, independent providers, and Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHC).​

What Project will be funded?

RHIN – $45 million

  • EHR upgrades/onboarding​
  • Network Infrastructure​
  • Care Quality Information Exchange ​
  • Analytics & Technical Assistance Hub​
  • Community Care Coordination Hub (C-Hub) ​
  • Duals Data Dashboard & Support Hub (DDDASH) ​

RVBI- $25 million

  • Statewide Federally Qualified Health Center (“FQHC”) Infrastructure, Analytics, Readiness Funding, and Transformation Support

(Notice of Award on 8/28 to Hawaii Primary Care Association)

Provide statewide support for Hawaiʻi FQHCs under the Rural Health Transformation Program. Services include EHR/EMR modernization and optimization, network and cybersecurity readiness, interoperability, provider-facing analytics, workflow and practice transformation, training and technical assistance, and administration of readiness funding to support participation in value-based care and AHEAD-aligned payment and delivery models.

  • Statewide Hospital Infrastructure, Analytics, Readiness Funding, and Transformation Support

(Notice of Award on 9/3 to Healthcare Association of Hawaii)

Provide infrastructure, analytics, readiness funding, and transformation support primarily for rural hospitals and, where applicable, associated care delivery organizations. Services include organizational readiness assessments; EHR/EMR modernization; interoperability, data quality, and analytics support; technical assistance; implementation coordination; and administration of approved Rural Value-Based Innovation funding. This federally funded work will strengthen rural health care infrastructure and support sustainable value-based care transformation throughout Hawaiʻi.

  • Clinically Integrated Network and Managed Services Organization Support

(Notice of Award on 9/11 to Physicians of Southwest Washington, LLC)

 Establish, operationalize, and support a statewide Clinically Integrated Network (“CIN”) and Managed Services Organization (“MSO”) shared-services model for eligible independent and community-based primary care providers and organizations. Services may include provider recruitment and onboarding; EHR/EMR and health information technology modernization; interoperability and analytics; care management and practice transformation; accountable-care and multi-payer readiness; financial operations; sustainability planning; and, if authorized, administration of SHPDA-approved Participation Support.
