Regional Grants Update

On Sept. 1, the first year of Grow Rural Opportunities for Well-being (GROW) Regional Grants were awarded, marking a significant step forward in Indiana's commitment in strengthening rural healthcare and improving health outcomes for Hoosiers.

The grants were awarded in eight regions where rural health partners joined together to create a unified plan to identify community needs and design local solutions.

Areas of focus include:

  • Pre- and post-natal care to improve maternal and infant health
  • Chronic disease prevention and management to conditions like diabetes, hypertension, obesity and related risks with evidence-based, outcomes-driven interventions
  • Access to care and removing barriers such as technology, transportation, healthy food, health education and connected referral pathways
These regional grants mark a major milestone in Indiana's long-term effort to support healthier families, expand access to essential services and build sustainable rural healthcare systems. Each of the eight regions developed a proposal reflecting the priorities of its community, with strategies highlighting local hospitals, community partners and local health departments.
 
This newly released funding will support initiatives and activities including:
  • Expanding prenatal and postpartum navigation, maternal fetal medicine access, infant support, prenatal mental health support and a rural-focused OB residency.
  • Providing new access points by expanding clinical capacity, adding primary care and behavioral health access points by launching mobile medical and dental services.
  • Building a regional rural health workforce pipeline with expanded recruitment, clinical training pathways, retention strategies and high-need positions.
  • Developing a coordinated regional transportation network linking hospitals, EMS, aging-services providers and local transit to reduce missed appointments and improve access to care.
  • Transforming behavioral health and crisis response through ED embedded clinicians and standardized regional crisis service messaging.
The regional grants work in tandem with 11 statewide RHTP Initiatives that continue to support innovation, strategic partnerships, infrastructure development and work force investment. Indiana's overall RHTP strategy directs 92% of the funding to about 230 Indiana organizations.

Resourcing local partners ensures solutions are driven by Hoosiers who understand local barriers, maintain trusted relationships, and have a vested interest in creating durable, long‑term improvements in rural health and the region’s economic vitality.

A 24/7 statewide hub improving rural access to critical care

- Preparing updates for Executive Steering Committee

Initiative 2: Expanding Indiana 211
Coordinated network linking rural residents to essential services
 
- Ongoing testing underway to improve agency efficiency
- Continuous coordination with partners to build a stronger system of care
 
Initiative 3: Enhanced Interoperability
Modernizing Health Information Exchange (HIE) facilities
 
- Two vendor contracts executed, beginning work
- Provider Readiness Assessment completed and under review

Initiative 4: Pediatric & OB Readiness
Strengthen emergency care capability for pediatric and OB emergencies
 
- Executed grant agreement with Indiana University School of Medicine
- Ongoing work with Indiana Department of Homeland Security

 Initiative 5: Cardiometabolic Standards
Expanding rural care to combat various cardiovascular diseases
 
- 10 Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) Centers of Excellence have been identified across all eight regions
- Food is Medicine vendor has been selected
Initiative 6: Meds-to-Beds
Program ensuring patients get medications before discharge
 
- 20 hospitals awarded funding for Budget Period One
- Kick-off meeting took place, introducing Advisory Committee

Initiatives 7 & 8: Telehealth & Teleconsult
Enhancing telehealth & teleconsult capability in rural communities
 
- Landscape Assessment in progress

Initiative 9: Growing Paraprofessional Workforce
