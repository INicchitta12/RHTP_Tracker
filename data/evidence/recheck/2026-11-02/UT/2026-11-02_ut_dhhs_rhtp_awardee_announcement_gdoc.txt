RHTP Awardee Announcement


The Utah Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) is preparing to formally announce the grant awardees for the Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP), supported by the $195,743,566 first-year award from CMS. 
In Utah’s RHTP several key activities have been contracted with a designated fiscal agent. A fiscal agent (also designated as a lead subrecipient, administrative partner, or consortium administrator) is an authorized state agency or contracted partner that receives primary grant allocations from Utah DHHS to provide fiscal stewardship, administrative oversight, and regulatory compliance.
Rather than expending all funds internally, Fiscal Agents administer competitive Requests for Applications (RFAs) and Requests for Proposals (RFPs). These open solicitations disburse subgrant funding directly to eligible rural hospitals, clinics, EMS providers, school districts, agricultural businesses, and local community organizations across all 25 designated rural Utah counties. Additionally, some lead partners will be soliciting interest from organizations to participate in activities such as community care hubs and value-based care models.
In the announcement below, entities designated as [Fiscal Agent & Open Subgrant Administrator] launch competitive funding calls or solicit participation interest and are open to all eligible rural applicants.
