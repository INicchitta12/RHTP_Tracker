RHT Year 1 Awards and Contracts

Updated as of October 30, 2026

The list below includes executed agreements for Vermont’s Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program Year 1 award. A description of Vermont’s RHT activities can be found here: https://healthcarereform.vermont.gov/document/rhtp-application-activity-details-12-2025. This list is updated weekly as agreements are finalized. As a result, recently executed agreements may not yet appear on this list. 

Update, October 30, 2026:  Vermont has fully obligated its approximately $195 million Year 1 RHT award. This means that the State has committed the full award and documented with CMS how the funding will be used.  

The list below list represents executed grant and contract agreements only and does not reflect the entirety of Vermont’s Year 1 RHT funding commitments. The list does not include activities that do not require individual grant or contract agreements, including certain provider payment methodologies and program administrative costs, as well as a small number of agreements that have been obligated but are not yet fully executed. We will continue to update this list weekly.  

The absence of an organization or activity from this list should not be interpreted as an indication that it was not selected. The State continues to consider proposals for Year 2 funding.
