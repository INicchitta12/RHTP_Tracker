​​​​​Our Plan, Updates and Progress

Relative Content

The Kentucky Rural Health Transformation Program (KY RHTP) is a five-year, federally funded initiative to strengthen health care in rural Kentucky. This page provides the program's plan, regular updates, and documentation of progress.

Kentucky's Plan

The program is guided by the application Kentucky submitted to the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). The original application and supporting documents are available below.​.
