Closed-Loop Community Care Platform

Oklahoma has implemented a closed-loop community care referral platform, “Findhelp,” which includes 4,800 different programs such as medical care, utilities, food support, and more. Funding will extend platform licenses to critical access hospitals, rural emergency hospitals, and FQHCs.

Funding Administrator:

Oklahoma Health Care Authority

Closed-Loop Community Care Platform Funding Recipients:

Findhelp, Inc., $830,400.00

Cohort 1 participants: Memorial Hospital of Texas County Authority, Great Plains Regional Medical Center, Beaver County Memorial Hospital, Harper County Community Hospital, DHR Duncan Regional, Okeene Municipal Hospital and Schallmo Authority

 

Additional hospitals and FQHCs to be onboarded in future cohorts – subject to change.
