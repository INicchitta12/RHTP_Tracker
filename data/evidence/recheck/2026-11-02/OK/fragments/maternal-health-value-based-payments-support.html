Maternal Health Value Based Payments Support

In conjunction with the CMS Transforming Maternal Health (TMaH) implementation, funding in this program will be used to issue incentives and bonuses for Medicaid fee-for-service (FFS) perinatal and maternal health providers who meet or exceed specific maternal health quality benchmarks. Funding will also support clinics in building the capabilities needed to participate in the TMaH value-based payment model and will eventually receive performance-based payments. 

Funding Administrator: Oklahoma Health Care Authority
Funded through interagency agreement
