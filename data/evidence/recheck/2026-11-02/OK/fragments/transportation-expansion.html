Transportation Expansion

Extending pilots that close transportation affordability and availability gaps to help rural residents access care

Funding Administrator:

Southwestern Oklahoma Development Authority (SWODA)

Funded through interagency agreement

Transportation Expansion Funding Recipients:

Southwestern Oklahoma Development Authority (SWODA) is providing $206,778.07 in mileage reimbursement for medical, health, and wellness trips for rural residents in Year 1.

SWODA also launched a competitive vehicle procurement open to OK DOT FTA Section 5311 Transit Agencies. The following 12 transit agencies were awarded funding for 20 vehicle purchases: 

  • Big Five - SORTS, $117,376.00 

  • Cherokee Strip, $240,000.00 

  • COCAA/COTS, $276,486.00 

  • Comanche Nations, $178,182.00 

  • First Capital Trolley, $297,447.00 

  • KATS, $203,595.90  

  • LIFT, $210,726.00 

  • Pelivan, $118,058.00 

  • Southwest Transit, $182,210.00 

  • United Community Action, $230,000.00
