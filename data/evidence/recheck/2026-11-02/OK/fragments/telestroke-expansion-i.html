Telestroke Expansion I

Enables real-time neurological consultations, improves transfer coordination, and increases the number of acute stroke-ready facilities across rural Oklahoma.

Telestroke Expansion Funding Recipients:

University of Oklahoma – Health Sciences Center (OUHSC), $499,555.00

Funded through interagency agreement

OUHSC’s Telestroke network is planned to include a total of 23 rural hospital sites.

Participating Sites:

Sites were identified based on health provider interest, readiness, population needs, and geographic distribution.

  • Atoka County Medical Center, Atoka, Atoka County

  • Coal County General Hospital, Coalgate, Coal County

  • Great Plains Regional Medical Center, Elk City, Beckham County

  • Harmon Memorial Hospital, Hollis, Harmon County

  • Holdenville General Hospital, Holdenville, Hughes County

  • Jackson County Memorial Hospital, Altus, Jackson County

  • Memorial Hospital of Texas County, Guymon, Texas County

  • Newman Memorial Hospital, Shattuck, Ellis County

  • Pushmataha Hospital, Antlers, Pushmataha County

  • Roger Mills Memorial Hospital, Cheyenne, Roger Mills County

  • Share Medical Center, Alva, Woods County

  • Cimarron Memorial Hospital, Boise City, Cimarron County

  • Eastern Oklahoma Medical Center, Poteau, Le Flore County

  • Stillwater Medical Center, Blackwell, Kay County

  • Stillwater Medical Center, Perry, Noble County

 

*Site implementation status is subject to change as contracting and deployment activities are completed. Additional sites will be posted once identified.
