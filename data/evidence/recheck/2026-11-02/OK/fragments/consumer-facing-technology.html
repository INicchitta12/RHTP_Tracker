Consumer-Facing Technology

This program will identify, evaluate, and deploy technology solutions that directly help rural Oklahomans access and engage with healthcare, including tools supporting prevention, screening, patient engagement, care navigation, self-management, behavioral health, maternal health, and other healthcare needs.

Funding Administrator:

Oklahoma State University – Center for Health Systems Innovation (CHSI)

Funded through interagency agreement.

Approximately 2–6 consumer-facing deployment sites/site partners are anticipated during the initial implementation period.
