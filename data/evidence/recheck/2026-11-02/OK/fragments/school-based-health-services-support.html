School-Based Health Services Support

The Oklahoma State Department of Education (OSDE) is funding 28 PK-12 districts through the PRIMS Project 695 grant for new health/mental health professional positions capable of billing for Medicaid services.

Funding Administrator:

Oklahoma State Department of Education (OSDE)

Funded through interagency agreement

 
Schools listed below were awarded funding for up to 90% of the salary and benefits for a health professional position. Examples include:

  • School nurses

  • Licensed counselors

  • Physical therapists

  • Occupational therapists

  • Speech‑language pathologists

Funding is contingent upon employees being appropriately credentialed and eligible to bill Medicaid for services provided in school settings. To support this process, OSDE is implementing EdPlan Health, a platform schools will use for billing. This will allow all appropriately licensed nurses, including LPNs, RNs and APRNs, to bill for eligible services.
