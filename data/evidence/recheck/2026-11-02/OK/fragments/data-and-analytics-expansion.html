Data and Analytics Expansion

This statewide initiative expands OKSHINE’s data and analytics capabilities to better identify rural health needs, care gaps, trends, and opportunities to target resources across Oklahoma.

The program will support development of data dashboards and analytics tools, improve data quality, expand health data reporting, and develop a citizen consent management portal.

Funding Administrator: Oklahoma Health Care Authority 
Funded through interagency agreement

This is a statewide data and analytics initiative and does not have designated participating sites.
