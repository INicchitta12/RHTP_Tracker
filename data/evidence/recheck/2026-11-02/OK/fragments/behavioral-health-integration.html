Behavioral Health Integration

Behavioral Health Integration – Medications for Opioid and Alcohol Use Disorder funding is intended to expand access to medications for opioid use disorder (MOUD) and medications for alcohol use disorder (MAUD) for rural communities through evidence-based provider upskilling and/or integrated/alternative care delivery models.

Oklahoma State University Center for Health Sciences (OSU-CHS) – SPARK Rural Health Addiction Medicine Expansion Program, $137,841.17 
Service Area: All 75 rural-designated Oklahoma counties. 
 
OSU-CHS will expand its SPARK Rural Health Addiction Medicine program to strengthen access to addiction medicine expertise across rural Oklahoma. The program will provide rural healthcare professionals with real-time consultation, education and training, referral resources, and ongoing support to help identify and treat substance use disorders, including opioid and alcohol use disorders.

Mercy Health Oklahoma Communities – Virtual Substance Use Recovery Program (vSURP), $90,171.53 
Service Area: 37 rural Oklahoma counties, with 44 physical entry points consisting of seven hospitals and 37 clinics. The 13 primary counties are Blaine, Canadian, Carter, Cleveland, Delaware, Garvin, Johnston, Kingfisher, Le Flore, Logan, McClain, Pontotoc, and Sequoyah, with an additional 24 surrounding rural counties served through the program. 
 
Mercy Health Oklahoma Communities will expand its Virtual Substance Use Recovery Program to improve access to treatment for alcohol use disorder in rural communities. Through a telehealth-enabled model, patients will be connected with medication-assisted treatment, behavioral health services, care coordination, and ongoing recovery support closer to home.

Andor Health, LLC, $1,000,000 
Service Area: Adair, Cherokee, Delaware, Mayes, McIntosh, Muskogee, Okmulgee, and Sequoyah counties. 
 
Andor Health will expand access to virtual addiction medicine services for individuals with opioid and alcohol use disorders in rural Oklahoma. The initiative will establish telehealth-enabled access points and provide technology-supported pathways to clinical care, medication treatment, provider support, and ongoing patient engagement.
