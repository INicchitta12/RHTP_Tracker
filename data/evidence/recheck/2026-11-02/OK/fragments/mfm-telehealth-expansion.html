MFM Telehealth Expansion

Funding expands access to maternal-fetal medicine and OB/GYN specialty care for rural patients through telemedicine and specialty-care partnerships.

MFM Telehealth Expansion Funding Recipients:

Funding Administrators:

Oklahoma State University – Center for Health Sciences (OSU-CHS) and the University of Oklahoma – Health Sciences Center (OUHSC)
Funded through interagency agreement

OSU-CHS plans to deploy MFM/OBGYN services to approximately two rural sites. Participating sites are currently being identified and will be posted once selection and implementation planning are complete.

OUHSC plans to deploy tele-MFM services to approximately 2 rural sites. Participating sites are currently being identified and will be posted here once selection and implementation planning are complete.
