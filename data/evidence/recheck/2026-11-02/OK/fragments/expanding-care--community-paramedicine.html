Funding was awarded to Hospitals, EMS services and Fire Departments for the purchase of vehicles to be used for the establishment and expansion of Community Paramedicine programs. Community paramedicine programs allow paramedics to work in partnership with hospitals and health care providers to provide care to people in their homes. 

Community paramedics receive additional medical training that allows them to provide basic primary care, chronic disease management, health education, and other services that improve health outcomes and prevent hospital readmissions. Community paramedicine funding awardees will also receive funding for technical assistance for program and operations development and community engagement. (Four vehicles were funded out of the NOFO funding; Nine were funded out of a separate funding opportunity reserved for teams trained by RHTP funds.)
