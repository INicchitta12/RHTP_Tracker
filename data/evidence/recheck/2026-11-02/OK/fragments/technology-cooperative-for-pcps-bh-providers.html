Technology Cooperative for PCPs/BH Providers

Funding will help rural primary care and behavioral health providers identify, access, and implement technology solutions designed to improve care delivery, provider efficiency, patient access, and long-term sustainability.

Funding Administrator:

Oklahoma State University – Center for Health Systems Innovation (CHSI)

Funded through interagency agreement.

The program will use a statewide recruitment, application, evaluation, and selection process to identify participating rural healthcare providers.

Approximately 20–35 rural provider organizations/sites are anticipated to participate during the initial implementation period.

Participating Providers:

Providers have not yet been selected. Selected organizations will be posted once the recruitment, application, evaluation, and selection process is complete.
