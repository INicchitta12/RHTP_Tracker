Remote Patient Monitoring: Maternal Health

Expanding on pilot programs for maternal health, Oklahoma Health Care Authority intends to implement a remote patient monitoring program utilizing blood pressure cuffs to complement State Center of Excellence models such as High-Risk Obstetrical Care Management (HROB) and CMS Transforming Maternal Health (TMaH).
