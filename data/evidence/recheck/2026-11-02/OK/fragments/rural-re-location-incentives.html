Rural Re-Location Incentives

Funding will be used as recruitment incentives for health care providers who relocate and establish full-time practice in rural areas. Providers commit to a five-year service obligation in exchange for up to $20,000 in relocation support and payment of $50,000 upon completion of each year of service. 

Funding Administrator: Healthcare Workforce Training Commission (HWTC)
Funded through interagency agreement
