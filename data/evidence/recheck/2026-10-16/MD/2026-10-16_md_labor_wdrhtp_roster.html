Rural Health Transformation Program - Division of Workforce Development and Adult Learning

About the Program

The Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) is helping Maryland strengthen the healthcare workforce in rural communities by expanding access to training, supporting healthcare employers, and building a stronger pipeline of healthcare professionals.

Maryland was awarded $168 million in federal funding for RHTP’s first budget period to strengthen and modernize healthcare in the 18 state-designated rural areas. The program is facilitated by the Maryland Department of Health, with workforce components coordinated by the Maryland Department of Labor.

In October 2026, MD Labor awarded a total of $16.4 million to 39 organizations through two initiatives:

Pathways to Health Careers

Pathways to Health Careers is designed for immediate impact, focusing on rapidly training Marylanders for entry-level and peer-supported healthcare roles. This initiative addresses shortages in allied health and community support occupations through two tracks:

  • Registered Apprenticeships that provide pathways into healthcare careers through paid, work-based training.
  • Rural Advancement for Maryland Peers (RAMP), which helps remove financial barriers to training and certification for in-demand occupations, including Community Health Workers and Certified Peer Recovery Specialists.

Rural Incentives for Strengthening Employment in Healthcare (RISE)

RISE helps rural communities recruit and retain healthcare professionals while building a pipeline of future talent. The initiative includes two tracks:

  • Training, recruitment, and retention of advanced practice professionals, including registered nurses, physician assistants, physicians, and dentists.
  • Career exploration and workforce development that connects students to healthcare career opportunities and helps incumbent workers upskill.

These initiatives are helping rural communities develop the skilled healthcare workforce they need now and in the future.
