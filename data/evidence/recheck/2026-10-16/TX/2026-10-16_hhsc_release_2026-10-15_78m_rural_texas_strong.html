Gov. Abbott, HHSC Announce Recipients of $78 Million in Rural Texas Strong Grants

News Release
Texas Health and Human Services Commission
Stephanie Muth
HHS Executive Commissioner
October 15, 2026
Emily Hummel, 512-424-6951
PressOffice@hhs.texas.gov

AUSTIN – Gov. Greg Abbott announced that HHSC plans to award $78 million in Rural Texas Strong grants to 216 rural health care providers throughout the state.
