THIS IS NOT SOUTH DAKOTA'S SUBAWARD LIST. SD has announced $31.5M to 28
projects (2026-07-23) and $90M to 82 rural healthcare organizations
(2026-08-19). NEITHER IS ON THIS PORTAL as of the fetch date above. The
register was searched on the RHT number series, on 'Rural Strong', on
'Rural Health Transformation', and with DOH's grants listed unfiltered
(463 rows, $39.4M, largest single row $2.6M and not RHTP). An
82-organisation, $90M round is not in that.

consulting, evaluation or workforce training. Real, primary, recipient-

rounds. Do not read the total as South Dakota's Tier 3 figure.
