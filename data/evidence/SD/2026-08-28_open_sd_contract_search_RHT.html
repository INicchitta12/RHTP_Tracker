Contract

Types of state contracts:

  • Service contracts are agreements to provide services instead of a supply or material and may be kept by the individual agency entering into the contract.
  • Consultant contracts are a subset of service contracts, used to obtain consulting services. Consultant contracts are required to be filed with the state auditor's office and copies can be obtained there.
  • Procurement contracts are issued by the Office of Procurement for supplies and materials and are available on the OPM website.
  • Legal contracts are filed with the attorney general's office.
