         Frequently Asked Questions: Grant Notifications
                                       September 29, 2026




1. I applied for CalRHT funding and saw the funding announcement. Does that
   mean my application was selected?
   No. The funding announcement provides a general update on CalRHT funding and
   does not confirm the outcome of an individual application. Applicants will receive
   information about their own application through the grants management system
   (Submittable).

2. I applied for CalRHT funding but have not received a notification about my
   application. What should I do?
   If you have not received a notification about your CalRHT application, please check
   the email address associated with your application, including your junk or spam
   folder.

3. I received a notice that my application was conditionally selected for CalRHT
   funding. What does that mean?
   A conditional selection means your application has been selected for CalRHT
   funding and, like all selected applicants, is subject to the completion of requests for
   information as outlined in your notification. Your award is not final until a subrecipient
   agreement is signed and executed.

4. My application met eligibility requirements and remains under consideration
   for a subaward. What does that mean?
   This means your application is still under review and a final funding decision has not
   yet been made. You will be notified regarding the status of your application by late
   October 2026. No action is required from you until further notice.

5. I received a notice that my application has been selected but is subject to
   modifications. What does that mean?
   An award subject to modifications means your application has been selected for
   CalRHT funding but requires revisions which are outlined in your notification email.

   Please review your notification carefully for details about the required modifications.
   You must respond to the changes by the stated deadline or the notice may be
   withdrawn. Revisions are to be submitted through the grants management system,
   Submittable.

6. What if my organization cannot proceed with the proposed changes?
   If your organization cannot accept the required modifications, HCAI may withdraw
   the Notice of Intent to Award and may offer funding to another applicant.

7. I received a notice that said my application was not selected. What does that
   mean?
   A non-selection notice means the application was not selected for funding during this
   budget period. This determination applies only to the current funding round; you may
   apply for future opportunities.

8. Will I receive details on the reasons for non-selection?
   To ensure a fair and consistent process for all applicants, we are unable to provide
   individualized details beyond the non-selection reason included in the notice, but we
   appreciate the time and effort each applicant invested in applying and encourage
   you to apply for future funding opportunities.

9. Once I receive my award notice, can I begin incurring expenses associated
   with my application?
   No. Do not incur expenses related to CalRHT funding until the subrecipient
   agreement is fully executed. Costs incurred beforehand are not eligible for
   reimbursement. Agreement terms must be met before funding payments can be
   issued.

10. What steps must be completed before a subrecipient agreement can be
    executed?
    Before a subrecipient agreement can be executed, applicants must complete the
    following:
        • All outstanding conditions must be met satisfactorily
        • Completion of HCAI’s Subrecipient Information and Risk Assessment
        • Completion of the subrecipient agreement using HCAI’s template
        • Ensure both parties execute the subrecipient agreement

11. What happens if I don’t respond by the stated deadline?
    If an applicant does not confirm their intent to proceed by the provided deadline,
    HCAI may withdraw the notice and offer funding to another applicant.

12. Can agreement terms change after an award?
    No. Once a subrecipient agreement has been signed, the terms under that
    agreement must be met for funding to be issued.

13. Will CalRHT help organizations find partners or implementation support?
   Yes. CalRHT has established a Rural Technical Assistance Center (RTAC) which
   connects eligible rural health organizations with technical assistance, training, peer-
   learning, and practical tools to support implementation of CalRHT-funded programs
