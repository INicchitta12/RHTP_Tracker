Rural  Arkansas  faces  major  challenges  across  de mography,  health  outco mes,  healthcare

Rural  Landscape  and  RHT  Progra m  Challenges  
About  45 %  of Arkansans  live in rural  areas,  making  it one  of the most  rural  states  in the 
U.S. [1][2] Rural co m munities tend to be older,  lower -inco me,  and  in poorer health than 
urban  residents,  with  li mited access to medical  services. [3][1] Nearly  half of rural  hospitals 
(50 %)  are  considered vulnerable  to closure — the  highest  rate  in the nation — with 79 % 
operating  at a loss  on  patient  services. [4] Rural  providers  face  significant  financial  strain 
due  to low  rei mburse ment rates,  as Arkansas  ranks in the  botto m  five nationally  in the 
Medicare Wage Index. [5] 
Rural  De mographics

• Population  density:  Typically,  fewer  than  30 people  per square  mile  in many

• Median  rural  household  inco me:  $50,210,  below  national  medians. [6]
