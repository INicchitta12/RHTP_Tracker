Appendix A – Summary of Initiatives

Rural Health Information Network (RHIN): A statewide digital backbone connecting rural hospitals, clinics, and health centers to the rest of the state through interoperable EHRs, wireless networks, and integrated data hubs.
Strategic Goals: Tech Innovation
Use of Funds: D, F, I, K
Technical Score Factors: B.1, B.2, C.1, E.2, C.2, E.1, F.2

Pili Ola Telehealth Network: A statewide telehealth network connecting rural communities to providers, integrating digital health access, virtual care, and telehealth training.
Strategic Goals: Tech Innovation; Sustainable Access
Use of Funds: A, B, C, D, F, G, H, K
Technical Score Factors: B.1, C.1, C.2, D.1, F.1, F.2, F.3

[PDF page 124, Appendix page 2]
Rural Infrastructure for Care Access (RICA): A physical access initiative expanding emergency medical services, community care, and behavioral health infrastructure in rural communities.
Strategic Goals: Make Rural America Healthy Again; Sustainable Access
Use of Funds: A, B, D, E, F, G, H, J, K
Technical Score Factors: B.1, B.2, C.1, C.2, D.1, F.1, F.2, F.3

Hawai'i Outreach for Medical Education in Rural Under-resourced Neighborhoods (HOME RUN): A pipeline initiative expanding education, recruitment, and retention of healthcare workers across rural Hawai'i through certificate programs, residencies, incentives, and mentoring.
Strategic Goals: Workforce development
Use of Funds: E, K
Technical Score Factors: C.1, D.1

[PDF page 125, Appendix page 3]
Rural Respite Network (RRN): A rural expansion of medical respite programs to reduce preventable hospital use among unhoused or post-acute patients with low medical acuity.
Strategic Goals: Sustainable access
Use of Funds: A, B, G, H, J, K
Technical Score Factors: B.1, B.2, C.1

Rural Value-Based Innovation (RVBI) & AHEAD Readiness Fund: A competitive fund enabling rural providers to adopt innovative care models and succeed under the AHEAD model by financing local value-based innovations.
Strategic Goals: Innovative care
Use of Funds: A, B, D, F, G, I, K
Technical Score Factors: B.1, B.2, C.1, E.1
