Nevada Rural Health System Flex Fund
March 2026



OVERVIEW
The Nevada Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program, as outlined in the state’s application, implements four new
initiatives to improve rural health outcomes and access to care. The RHT program is a five-year cooperative agreement
between the Nevada Health Authority (NVHA) and the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS).

NEVADA RURAL HEALTH SYSTEM FLEX FUND

$35.9 million will be available in Budget Period 1 (BP1) to fund capital expenditures and equipment that will enhance
and modernize local rural health care infrastructures. Funds are intended to modernize and enhance existing facilities and
infrastructure for Nevada’s rural healthcare system including capital and infrastructure expenditures for rural clinics,
hospitals, EMS, non-emergency medical transportation for rural care, community health programs, mobile care facilities,
medical equipment and supplies, lab and testing technologies. Projects can support resources to improve revenue cycle
management. Projects should increase access to remote care, increase the number of mobile units in rural Nevada, and
increase transport access for non-emergency and emergency services in rural areas.

Distribution of Funds: The Nevada Health Authority will create a request for application (RFA) for eligible health care
providers and entities to access these funds. Sub-recipients must be registered vendors with the State of Nevada we
encourage entities to begin the registration process at NV Vendor services. The first RFA is planned for release in March of
2026 and will remain open until August 28, 2026 or until all funds are distributed.

Funds awarded under these subgrants in BP1 must be obligated by October 30, 2026, and liquidated (i.e., fully spent) by
September 30, 2027.

Funding Limitations:
There is a 10% cap on all RHT funding that may be used for administrative costs, including both indirect and direct costs.
As noted above, maintenance plans may be included in requesting RHT funds to support associated expenditures under
Nevada’s rural health system flex fund; and broadband infrastructure is an unallowable cost under the RHT Program. RHT
funds may not be used for new construction or major building expansions, though minor alterations and renovations tied
to program goals may be permitted (see CMS RHTP FAQ #13).
