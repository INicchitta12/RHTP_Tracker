Nevada Workforce Recruitment and Rural Access Program
March 2026



OVERVIEW
The Nevada Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program, as outlined in the state’s application, implements four new
initiatives aimed at improving rural health outcomes and access to care. The RHT program is a five-year cooperative
agreement between the Nevada Health Authority (NVHA) and the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS).

WORKFORCE RECRUITMENT & RURAL ACCESS PROGRAM (WRRAP)

$71.9 million will be available in Budget Period 1 (BP1) to attract and retain physicians and a wide variety of
other health care provider types in rural areas of the state with specific retention strategies to ensure WRRAP recipients
serve rural Nevada. Strong preference for awards related to financial incentives and training assistance programs will be
given to rural hospitals and health care providers that operate in rural counties such as rural hospitals, certified community
behavioral health centers, federally-qualified health centers, primary and behavioral health providers, school health
services programs, school-based health centers, and other health clinics. Proposals may be entertained from other entities
including academic institutions for funding to support the creation of a Rural Physician Residency Program and for
additional training assistance programs that will serve rural communities in Nevada. NVHA may also consider proposals
from other non-rural hospitals and healthcare systems that seek to support rural training programs for rural provider
systems if such proposals are supported by strong letters of support from local or tribal health officials, rural hospitals,
and other health care providers of services located in the community impacted by the proposal. All projects funded
through WRRAP will be tied to a 5-year rural service commitment.

Distribution of Funds: WRRAP will include three separate funding opportunities. WRRAP projects will be supported
through the request for proposal (RFP) and request for application (RFA) processes as outlined below. Subrecipients and
contract vendors must be registered vendors with the State of Nevada. NVHA encourages interested entities to begin the
registration process now at NV Vendor services. Future RFPs and RFAs will be revised annually to reflect best practices
and will be issued upon CMS’ continued approval of Nevada’s RHT Funds. Funds awarded under these subawards or
contracts in BP1 must be obligated by October 30, 2026, and liquidated (i.e., fully spent) by September 30, 2027.

1. Rural Medical Residency Programs
    • Process: Request for Proposal (RFP)
    • Funding Opportunity Release: May 2026
    • Administered by: NVHA and Nevada State Purchasing Division
2. Rural workforce - Provider Recruitment and Retention
    • Process: Request for application (RFA)
    • Funding Opportunity Release: April 2026
    • Administered by: NVHA
3. Rural Workforce - Provider Training and Apprenticeships
    • Process: Request for application (RFA)
    • Funding Opportunity Release: April 2026
    • Administered by: NVHA

Funding limitations:
There is a 10% cap on funding that can be used for administrative costs, including both indirect and direct costs.
RHT program funding may not be used to directly fund clinician salaries or workforce development initiative where the
clinicians or initiative beneficiaries are subject to a non-compete agreement. As noted above, funding for local housing
for students is limited to 6 months for rotations.
