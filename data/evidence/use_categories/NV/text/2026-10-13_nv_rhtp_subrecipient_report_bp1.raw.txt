Rural Health Transformation Program Year 1 Funding Amounts
On December 29, 2025, the State of Nevada initiated the Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP)
following the initial federal grant award of $179,931,608.42 from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid
Services (CMS). Authorized under H.R.1 Section 71401 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, this five-year
federal grant is designed to strengthen rural healthcare systems and mitigate the impacts of anticipated
changes to Medicaid financing for rural hospitals and providers.
To ensure strategic and effective allocation of these Budget Period 1 (BP1: 12/29/25 – 10/30/26) funds,
the Nevada Health Authority issued multiple Requests for Applications (RFAs), directing investments
toward four core initiatives and four designated focus areas.
Core RHTP Initiatives
1. Rural Health Outcome Accelerator Program (RHOAP)
2. Nevada Rural Health System Flex Fund (FLEX)
3. Workforce Recruitment and Rural Access Program (WRRAP)
4. Rural Health Innovation and Technology (RHIT)
Targeted RHTP Funding
1. Rural Tribal Health Transformation
2. Rural Veterans Health Transformation
3. Rural Corrections Health Transformation
4. Presidential Fitness Test Implementation
This report provides a comprehensive overview of all projects funded during BP1 across both core
initiatives and targeted RHTP funding categories. Note: The following list of subaward amounts may not
be final as project budgets continue to be refined for CMS approval at the time of this writing (early
October 2026).
Rural Health Outcome Accelerator Program (RHOAP)
RHOAP funding supports initiatives that advance value-based care and innovative healthcare delivery
models aimed at preventing and managing chronic disease. Through a single RFA, the Nevada Health

Authority awarded funds to subrecipients committed to expanding access through hybrid and integrated
care models; implementing targeted interventions to address chronic disease, primary and preventive
care, behavioral health, and maternal and infant health; and incentivizing providers who demonstrate

Nevada Rural Health System Flex Fund (FLEX)
The Nevada Rural Health System Flex Fund is a core component of Nevada’s Rural Health
Transformation Program. This initiative provides essential financial support for capital investments and
equipment modernization to strengthen rural healthcare infrastructure. FLEX resources are intended to
increase facility capacity, enhance service delivery, and improve long-term sustainability for rural
hospitals and healthcare providers statewide. Funding requests totaling $180.7 million from 103
applicants far exceeded the published $35.9 million available for this initial year of funding. Funding was
strictly prioritized for direct, frontline rural health care facilities and providers and all applications were
scored independently based on the matrix provided in the RFA. NVHA final decisions strictly followed
these competitive merits. The funded projects address urgent unmet needs and pent-up demands for
our rural and frontier hospitals and systems of care. This included significant gaps in medical
technology, equipment, facilities, emergency and life-saving equipment, and mobile care in rural
Nevada.

Workforce Recruitment and Rural Access Program (WRRAP)
WRRAP is the largest-funded initiative within the Rural Health Transformation Program, designed to
expand and sustain a robust rural healthcare workforce. To address historic provider shortages, the
Nevada Health Authority released three targeted RFAs:
4070 Silver Sage Drive ● Carson City, Nevada 89701
Page 10 of 24
• WRRAP Recruitment and Retention
• WRRAP Apprenticeship and Training
• WRRAP Rural Workforce Projects
These coordinated efforts aim to strengthen provider pipelines, expand training opportunities, and
support long-term workforce stability throughout rural Nevada.
WRRAP: Provider Recruitment and Retention (R+R)
WRRAP Provider Recruitment and Retention Program received funding requests totaling $41.9 million
from 38 applicants, thus exceeding the published $32.3 million available for this initial year of funding.
The funded projects address critical healthcare workforce shortages across rural Nevada, including gaps
in primary care, behavioral health, dental services, specialty care, emergency medical services, and
provider recruitment and retention in frontier and Tribal communities. Investments focus on recruiting
and retaining primary care providers, behavioral health professionals, dental providers, nurses,
emergency medical personnel, and other essential healthcare workers needed to sustain and expand
local access to care.

Rural Health Innovation and Technology (RHIT)
RHIT funding supports innovative, technology-driven solutions that improve access to healthcare in rural

technologies in secure, interoperable, and future-ready formats. Funding requests totaling $33 million
from 36 applicants exceeded the published $26.9 million available for this initial year of funding.

in remote patient monitoring, in patient care and improve access to care coordination through patient
portals.
