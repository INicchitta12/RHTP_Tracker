Nevada Rural Health Outcome Acclerator Program
March 2026



OVERVIEW
The Nevada Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program, as outlined in the state’s application, implements four new
initiatives aimed at improving rural health outcomes and access to care. The RHT program is a five-year cooperative
agreement between the Nevada Health Authority (NVHA) and the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS).

RURAL HEALTH OUTCOME ACCELERATOR PROGRAM (RHOAP)

$26.9 million will be available in Budget Period 1 (BP1) to fund evidence-based initiatives intended to
improve rural health in key areas of:
   • Chronic Disease: preventable cancers, diabetes, chronic lower respiratory disease, and heart disease;
   • Primary Care: access and preventable services, including oral health;
   • Behavioral Health: mental illness, suicide, and substance use among pregnant women and youth; and
   • Maternal and Infant Health: prenatal care.

Distribution of Funds: The Nevada Health Authority in coordination with the Nevada State Purchasing Division will create a
request for proposal (RFP) slated for release in May 2026. Vendors responding under this proposal must be registered
vendors with the State of Nevada. NVHA encourages interested entities to begin the registration process now at NV Vendor
services. Future RFPs will be revised annually to reflect best practices and will be issued upon CMS’ continued approval of
Nevada’s RHT Funds. Funds awarded under these contracts in BP1 must be obligated by October 30, 2026, and liquidated
(i.e., fully spent) by September 30, 2027.

Funding Limitations:
There is a 10% cap on all RHT funding that may be used for administrative costs, including both indirect and direct costs.
