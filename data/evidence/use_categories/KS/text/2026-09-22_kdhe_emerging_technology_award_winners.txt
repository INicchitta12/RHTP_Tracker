      Operating as a regional technology deployment hub in cooperation with the
       Sumner County Educational Services Interlocal 619, this initiative scales a
       proven, university-developed Extended Reality (XR) software solution (the

       education workflows. The project establishes a scalable, decentralized access
       model that addresses critical regional workforce shortages, eliminates multi-
