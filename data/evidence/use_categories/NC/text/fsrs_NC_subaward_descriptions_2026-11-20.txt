=== subaward ACCESSEAST0101 | action 2026-06-16 | amount 14250000.00
THE NC RURAL HEALTH TRANSFORMATION PROGRAM HAS ESTABLISHED NORTH CAROLINA RURAL ORGANIZATIONS ORCHESTRATING TRANSFORMATION FOR SUSTAINABILITY (NC ROOTS) HUBS. EACH NC ROOTS HUB WILL ESTABLISH AND COORDINATE A REGIONAL NETWORK OF EXPERTS AND PARTNERS TO ADVANCE PROGRAM INITIATIVES. THIS NETWORK MAY INCLUDE RURAL SAFETY NET PROVIDERS, BEHAVIORAL HEALTH PROVIDERS, COMMUNITY-BASED ORGANIZATIONS, ACADEMIC INSTITUTIONS, COMMUNITY MEMBERS, AND OTHER STAKEHOLDERS WORKING COLLABORATIVELY TO DESIGN AND IMPLEMENT RURAL HEALTH IMPROVEMENT PROJECTS THAT RESPOND TO THE NEEDS OF RURAL RESIDENTS. GRANTEES SHALL PARTNER WITH NCDHHS TO FURTHER NCRHTP’S THREE GOALS FOR RURAL COMMUNITIES: 1. CATALYZE INNOVATIVE CARE MODELS, CHANGING THE WAY PROVIDERS WORK TOGETHER TO CARE FOR PATIENTS IN RURAL NC; 2. TRANSFORM THE RURAL CARE EXPERIENCE, BUILDING IMPROVED SYSTEMS OF COMMUNITY-BASED MEDICAL, BEHAVIORAL, AND SOCIAL SUPPORTS CLOSE TO HOME; AND 3. CREATE A SUSTAINABLE RURAL DELIVERY SYSTEM THROUGH UNDERLYING SYSTEMS CHANGE IN RURAL WORKFORCE PIPELINES AND CARE TEAM MODELS, AND RURAL PROVIDER FINANCIAL MODELS. ACCESS EAST SERVES AS THE ROOTS HUB LEAD FOR NC REGION 6.
