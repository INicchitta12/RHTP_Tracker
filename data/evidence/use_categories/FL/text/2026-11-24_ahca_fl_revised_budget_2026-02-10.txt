       BUDGET NARRATIVE: FLORIDA RURAL HEALTH TRANSFORMATION


Introduction and Summary
This Budget Narrative covers the five-year performance period, Federal FY 2026 – FY 2030 (the
“project period”) and follows CMS’s Guidance for Preparing a Budget Request and Narrative.
Throughout this document, we present a year-by-year breakdown for each cost category for the
entire period of performance. In the remainder of this application, we label years as follows:
Year 1 = FY26, Year 2 = FY27, Year 3 = FY28, Year 4 = FY29, and Year 5 = FY30.
Per CMS instruction, the budget is developed at the hypothetical $200,000,000 per-year level
(total $1,000,000,000 over five years); final awards may differ, and the Florida Agency for
Health Care Administration (Agency) will rescale initiatives post-award without eliminating or
adding initiatives outside the scope of this application. The budget has been updated to reflect
the approved Year 1 allocation of $209,938,194.50.
How to Read This Document:
   •   Annual Breakdown: Each cost-category (A-J) provides the annual cost for all five years,
       in compliance with CMS guidance.
   •   Initiative ID: Each sub-section is explicitly titled with the Initiative number (e.g., “F.1.
       Initiative 1: Rural and Satellite Clinics”) to link each cost directly to the programmatic
       framework.

Award Process: To award funds to applicants, the Agency grouped Florida into four “Super
Regions” designed to preserve existing partnerships that rural providers may have with urban
centers. Florida’s RHT Program award will be allocated to these four regions based on the
percentage of Florida’s rural population in each super region. Each RHT Program initiative will
be allocated funding amounts within each super region proportional to the super region’s rural
population. The Agency may reserve the option to issue statewide awards when such investments
promote efficiency and shared benefit across all regions, when sufficient regional initiatives are
not proposed to the Agency.
To distribute RHT Program funds awarded to Florida, the Agency intends to issue a Request for
Application (RFA) to notify potential applicants of the available funding. The RFA is a
competitive process designed to ensure fair and open competition for public funds. To maximize
the impact of RHT Program funds, the Agency will only accept applications from applicants that
have formed regional collaboratives within each super region with a single, cohesive, proposal
designed to meet the needs of the rural area for which the regional collaborative represents. Each
regional collaborative will be comprised predominately of rural providers, with a single entity
nominated to represent the consortium. Examples of eligible provider types include hospitals,
FQHCs, RHC, practitioners, and pharmacies, among others. Each regional collaborative’s
proposal must include, at a minimum, initiatives related to Prevention & Chronic Disease,
Consumer Tech Solutions, and Workforce strategies. Due to the size of the four super regions,
the Agency may issue awards to multiple applicants within a super region. For all contractors,
solicitations will specify:
   •   Scope of Work: Clear deliverables, performance metrics, and milestones tied directly to
       the Project Narrative
   •   Selection Criteria: Evaluation criteria will include but not be limited to: demonstrated
       organizational and fiscal capacity to manage a large-scale federal program; experience
       and existing partnerships with Florida’s rural providers, hospitals, and CBOs; a robust
       plan for data collection and reporting; strategies for health equity; and cost-
       reasonableness.
   •   Accountability: Contracts will include requirements for standardized quarterly data
       submission to the Agency’s Data Dashboard, performance-based payments, and
       corrective action remedies.
