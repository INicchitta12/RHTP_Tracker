Live discussion: readiness, activity changes, and budget decisions


          Scenario 1: Onboarding Readiness
          During onboarding, your team realizes that staff roles are unclear and required data collection
          processes have not been established.

          Scenario 2: Proposed Activity Change
          Your needs assessment suggests replacing an approved training activity with a different
          approach that may better meet local needs.

          Scenario 3: Budget Adjustment
          Your team wants to shift funds from one approved activity to another to address an emerging
          need.

Core Practice: Recommended Next Steps

 1. Onboarding Readiness           TA Support
 TA can help clarify roles and develop processes.



 2. Proposed Activity Change       TA Support + AHCA Guidance
 TA can help assess and document the proposed change, but AHCA should determine
 whether approval is required.


 3. Budget Adjustment              AHCA Guidance; approval may be required
 Seek AHCA guidance before making the change; formal approval may be required.
