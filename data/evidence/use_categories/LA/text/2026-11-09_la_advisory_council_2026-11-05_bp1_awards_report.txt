      Use of Funds – Caps on Funding
There are four categories for funding that have limitations on the amount that can be allocated.

•   Capital expenditures and infrastructure funding cannot exceed 20% of the total funding CMS awards States in a
    given budget period.

•   Funding for provider payments cannot exceed 15% of the total funding CMS awards States in a given budget
    period.

•   No more than 5% of total funding CMS awards to a State in a given budget period can support funding the
    replacement of an EMR system if a previous HITECH certified EMR system is already in place as of September 1,
    2025.

•   Funding towards initiatives similar to the “Rural Tech Catalyst Fund Initiative” cannot exceed the lesser of (1) 10%
    of total funding awarded to a State in a given budget period or (2) $20M of total funding awarded to a State in a
    given budget period, and funding is subject to all restrict

      Funding Definitions
•   LDH Awarded Amount
     Amount of funding LDH will fund based on sub-awardees application
     Budget amount may be modified as work through contracting process

•   LDH Fully Executed Contract
     Contract / Agreement has been signed by sub-awardee, LDH Secretary / designee, and approved by the Office of
       State Procurement

•   CMS Obligated Funds
     CMS accepting good faith documentation of a State’s intent to establish a financial obligation for purposes of
      meeting October 30, 2026, obligation deadline. States may subsequently finalize agreements or reallocate funds to
      other contracts, activities, or funding categories, subject to applicable grants management requirements and
      approvals.

•   Expended Funds
     All Budget Period 1 awarded / contracted funds must be expended by September 30, 2027.
     Sub-awardees will be required to submit invoicing with appropriate documentation prior to payment being made.
     All agreements will have designated contract monitor to work collaboratively and ensure appropriate regulations
       are met.
