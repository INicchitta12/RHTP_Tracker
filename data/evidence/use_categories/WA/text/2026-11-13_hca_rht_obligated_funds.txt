Washington’s RHT funds are 100% obligated
Earlier this year, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) awarded Washington state about
$181 million for year 1 of the Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program. As of this week, these funds are
100% obligated, which means CMS legally commits to providing all the funding.
The money is now at work, reaching all 30 of Washington's rural counties and serving 1.1 million rural
residents.

Where the money is
Washington’s RHT Program has six initiatives that support health care innovation in rural communities.
Each was built with rural partners and approved by CMS. Here’s how year 1 funds were shared.
•     Initiative 1: Ignite innovation in rural hospitals
•     Initiative 2: Prevent disease and manage care
•     Initiative 3: Invest in the health of Native families
•     Initiative 4: Adopt technology and data solutions
•     Initiative 5: Develop Washington's rural workforce
•     Initiative 6: Expand rural behavioral health
•     Administrative costs

Initiative 1: Ignite innovation in rural hospitals
Initiative 1 focuses on sustainable improvements in rural hospitals. This includes technology that supports
better collaboration between 30 rural hospitals, building capacity to share staff and tools and support peer
learning.

Initiative 3: Invest in the health of Native families
Washington state set aside 10% of funding for the 29 federally recognized Tribes in Washington. Each Tribe
receives the same amount, approximately $669,000 each, and determines how to best make critical
investments to improve the health and wellbeing of Native families.

Initiative 6: Expand rural behavioral health
This initiative improves standards for behavioral health care and gets people into care faster. This includes
expanding schools’ access to Medicaid funding, with 32 school districts participating this year.

Administrative costs
To support running Washington’s RHT Program, including completing 144 total contracts to move funds,
we've currently budgeted 5% of funding for administration. CMS allows states up 10% of the total funding for
administration.

Learn more about the RHT Program
The RHT Program is part of the H.R.1 budget reconciliation bill. The program offers $50 billion in funding
nationwide to improve rural health care. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) administers
the program.
