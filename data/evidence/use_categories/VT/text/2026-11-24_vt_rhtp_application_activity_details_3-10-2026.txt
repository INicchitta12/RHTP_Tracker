                          Vermont’s Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program Application Activity Details

                                                         Posted March 4th, 2026



Vermont’s publicly posted narrative (and summary) describe the various work the state plans to accomplish through the federal RHT
Program funding opportunity. Vermont’s RHT Program vision will be executed through thirty grant opportunities (also called subrecipients)
as well as procurement of two consultants and four contractors (including an independent evaluator). The state’s key initiatives include: (1)
Regionalization and Innovative Care Strategies (“Regionalization”); (2) Establishing a Clinically Integrated Network of Shared Services
(“Shared Services”); (3) Strengthening Primary Care (“Primary Care”); (4) Health Care Workforce Development (“Workforce Development”);
and (5) Price Transparency and Insurance Competition. A note that the items listed in the tables below are subject to change pending
forthcoming negotiations with the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services.
