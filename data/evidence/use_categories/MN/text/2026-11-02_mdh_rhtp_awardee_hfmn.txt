Rural Health Transformation Program Funding
K-12 HEALTHCARE CAREER EXPOSURE AND EARN &
TRAIN PATHWAYS
      Organization       City      Amount                       Purpose
HealthForce MN       Winona     $654,773    Introduce K-12 student to heath care careers
                                            through Scrubs Camps and HOSA future
                                            health professional chapters. These are two
                                            evidence-based programs that introduce
                                            rural high school students to high-demand
                                            careers such as nursing assistant, community
                                            health worker, community paramedic, EMT,
                                            dental assistant, and behavioral health
                                            careers. HealthForce will offer two rural
                                            Scrubs Camps (one new camp and an
                                            expansion of another camp) and six new
                                            HOSA chapters annually.
                                            Allied health earn and train pathways to
                                            support career growth opportunities for rural
                                            workers to become apprentices and gain
                                            training and credentials for entry-level health
                                            care roles while working for rural health care
                                            employers such as clinics, hospitals, Tribal
                                            health, and FQHCs. Employees will be able to
                                            work part-time, and employers will use grant
                                            funds to cover the costs of time for classes
                                            and studying, tuition, and other related costs
                                            of training employees for high-demand
                                            health care.
