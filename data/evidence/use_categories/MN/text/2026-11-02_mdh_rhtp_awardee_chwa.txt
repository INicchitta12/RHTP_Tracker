Rural Health Transformation Program Funding
EARN & TRAIN PATHWAYS
      Organization             City      Amount                        Purpose
Minnesota Community      Saint Paul   $386,146    Work with rural health care employers to
Health Worker Alliance                            support career growth opportunities for rural
                                                  workers to become community health
                                                  workers (CHWs) and community health
                                                  representative (CHRs). These apprentices will
                                                  gain training and credentials for entry-level
                                                  health care roles while working for rural
                                                  health care employers such as clinics,
                                                  hospitals, Tribal health facilities, FQHCs, local
                                                  public health, and community-based
                                                  organizations.
                                                  MNCHWA will provide employers with
                                                  technical assistance to support CHW
                                                  apprenticeships at their site as well as
                                                  integrating CHW positions into the care team.
                                                  Employees will be able to work part-time and
                                                  will receive student support funds from
                                                  MNCHWA throughout their education and
                                                  apprenticeship program. Employers will
                                                  receive support from MNCHWA’s grant funds
                                                  to cover the costs of time for classes and
                                                  studying, and other related costs of training
                                                  employees for high-demand health care
                                                  fields. Tuition will be paid with grant funds by
                                                  MNCHWA directly to educational institutions.
