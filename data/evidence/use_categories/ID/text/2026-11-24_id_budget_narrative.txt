                                                                                                                                                                   1
Idaho proposes a time-limited RHTP team, staffed by limit-service positions for the duration of the award period. This personnel
team will provide the strategic, operational, fiscal, and clinical oversight needed to manage a complex, statewide cooperative
agreement. Positions include a Project Director (Project Manager 3) to lead implementation and contractor performance and to
coordinate cross-agency activities; an Administrative Assistant and Technical Records Specialist will support logistics and
communications; Financial Specialist positions (3) will oversee compliance, reporting, fiscal controls, and general budget
maintenance; the Principal position will provide fiscal team oversight. The Epidemiologist will focus on enhancements to data
systems, data analytics, and workflows. The Idaho Department of Health & Welfare Director will also provide oversight to ensure
synergy across strategies and contracts. This time will be provided in-kind at the state's cost.
Specialized staff manage initiative clusters:
  · Access & Infrastructure: Project Manager will coordinate facility and technology modernization.
  · Workforce: Project Manager will oversee recruitment, training, and retention initiatives.
  · Innovative Care Technology: Project Manager will guide telehealth, data modernization, and cybersecurity projects.
  · Evidence-Based Projects: Program Manager will ensure interventions meet federal and state standards.
  · Grants/Contracts Officers (2): develops and manages procurement and solicitations including RFPs and award documentation.
  · Grants/Contracts Specialists (2): administer subgrants and contracts; provide compliance guidance and technical assistance.

Accountability: Each position has a defined scope of work and performance objectives. Effort is certified per pay period on
individual timesheets and deliverables are linked to project milestones subject to administrative and fiscal review.
