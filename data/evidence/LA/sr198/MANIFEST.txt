LOUISIANA -- SENATE RESOLUTION 198 (2026 RS) AND ITS 2026-10-15 REPORT (session 99)

SR 198 by Sen. Mizell, adopted 2026-06-01 (36-0), 'urge[s] and request[s]' LDH to
submit to the SENATE '(1) No later than October 15, 2026, a report that provides the
proposed program expenditures for federal Fiscal Year 2026-2027' and '(2) No later than
November 1, 2027' actual FY2026-27 and proposed FY2027-28 expenditures, each to
'include identification of recipients of funding and the amount of funding allocated'.
It names no committee and NO POSTING LOCATION. R.S. 24:772(A)(1) has the agency
e-mail the report to the David R. Poynter Legislative Research Library, which lists
it to members -- distribution, not publication. LDH lists its legislative reports,
newest first and named by their requesting instrument, on its 'Legislative &
Governmental Relations' resources category: R/03ae's 'leg_reports' probe key.
