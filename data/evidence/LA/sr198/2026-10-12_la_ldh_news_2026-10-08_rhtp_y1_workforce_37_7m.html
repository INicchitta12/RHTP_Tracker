  • Louisiana invests $37.7 million to strengthen rural health care workforce

October 08, 2026 Louisiana invests $37.7 million to strengthen rural health care workforceYear 1 funding supports education, training, recruitment, and retention in rural communities

Baton Rouge, Louisiana, Oct 08, 2026 - The Louisiana Department of Health (LDH) today announced up to $37.7 million in Year 1 workforce investments through the Rural Health Transformation Program to expand health care training opportunities and help rural providers recruit and retain the professionals they need.

Funded by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the investments connect Louisiana’s public universities and community and technical colleges with rural hospitals, clinics, and other health care providers to build a stronger workforce pipeline in rural communities.

“Access to health care starts with having the right people in the right places,” said LDH Secretary Bruce D. Greenstein. “These investments address the rural workforce challenge from multiple directions — helping Louisianans train for health care careers, connecting that training to real workforce needs, and giving rural providers additional tools to recruit and retain clinicians. We are building a workforce that can support better access to care in rural Louisiana for years to come.”

The $37.7 million investment includes three workforce initiatives:

Rural HERO — up to $20 million

The Rural Healthcare Employment Reinvestment Opportunity program will support campus-level programs through Louisiana’s four public postsecondary systems: the LSU System, Southern University System, University of Louisiana System, and Louisiana Community and Technical College System. Each participating campus will partner with at least one rural health care provider, connecting education and training programs with workforce needs in rural communities. Programs will begin in October 2026 and continue through September 30, 2029.

Rural Clinician Credit Bank — up to $12.7 million

LDH is awarding up to $12.7 million through the Rural Clinician Credit Bank to help rural health care providers recruit and retain physicians, nurses, dentists, EMS personnel, behavioral health professionals, and other clinicians. Fifty-three providers were selected for funding from 136 applications.

The program provides matching funds for recruitment and retention incentives, including sign-on and retention bonuses, relocation assistance, and professional training.

Grow Your Own — up to $5 million

The Grow Your Own initiative, administered through the Louisiana Community and Technical College System, will create health care career pathways across 39 campuses. The program is designed to reach students and workers at different stages through three pathways:

  • Discover: Dual-enrollment opportunities that allow high school students to begin health care coursework before graduation.
  • Earn: Short-term credential programs that prepare participants to enter the health care workforce.
  • Advance: Stackable education and training pathways that allow health care workers, including certified nursing assistants and licensed practical nurses, to advance toward higher-level credentials while remaining in the workforce.

“These investments are designed to work together,” said Julie Foster Hagan, executive director of the Office of Rural Health Transformation and Sustainability. “We are creating more opportunities for people to train for health care careers close to home, connecting education directly to the needs of rural providers, and helping those providers recruit and retain the workforce they need. Building those connections is critical to creating a stronger, more sustainable rural health care system.”

Year 2 funding opportunities are expected to open in November 2026.

About the Rural Health Transformation Program

Louisiana received more than $208 million in Year 1 funding through the Rural Health Transformation Program. The program is administered by the Office of Rural Health Transformation and Sustainability within LDH and supports efforts to improve access to care, strengthen rural health care systems, expand workforce capacity, and modernize technology and infrastructure.

For more information about Louisiana’s Rural Health Transformation Program, visit the LDH Rural Health Transformation Program webpage or email [email protected].
