Trump Administration Announces $167 Million to Build Rural Care Sites, Upgrade Health Technology, and Strengthen Prevention Across South Carolina 

This federal investment will support telehealth, remote monitoring, mobile care, cybersecurity upgrades, and new technology to improve maternal and behavioral healthcare 

The Trump Administration announced today that a $167 million investment is being delivered to expand local access, modernize technology and infrastructure, improve prevention, and strengthen workforce capacity for South Carolinians through the federal Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP). This investment will give residents more ways to access primary care, maternal and infant health, pediatrics, wellness, and behavioral health through new and modernized care locations, mobile units, telehealth, and other healthcare technologies such as local 24-hour pharmacy kiosks.  

The RHTP funding will support 228 grants to reduce barriers to care and strengthen healthcare access in rural communities through pop-up healthcare locations, mobile vans, new clinics, and school-based mental health services. Funded projects will modernize health IT infrastructure, including electronic health records, digital intake, and remote monitoring to manage chronic conditions. Specific IT projects include cybersecurity upgrades for a rural hospital and a specialized obstetrical surgical robot designed to improve outcomes for mothers and babies during complicated deliveries.

Today’s announcement is one part of the larger overall funding amount awarded to South Carolina for fiscal year 2026. 

Key initiatives this overall RHTP funding will focus on include: 

  • Connections to Care: Expanding electronic health records, telehealth, remote patient monitoring, and other digital tools to improve access and care coordination. 
  • Leveling Up: Scaling proven approaches in chronic disease management, pediatric care, and workforce development across rural communities. 
  • Wellness Within Reach: Expanding mobile health services, crisis response, pop-up clinics, and other approaches that bring care closer to rural residents.
  • Shoring Up Sustainability: Supporting workforce recruitment, facility upgrades, provider training, and maternal healthcare partnerships to strengthen rural providers. 

The Trump Administration is delivering on its commitment to strengthen rural America by investing in solutions that modernize technology to promote health and well-being for rural communities. Led by CMS, the RHTP is moving beyond the failed one-size-fits-all approach and giving rural communities the tools to build stronger, more innovative, and more sustainable health systems that deliver better care and better outcomes for the Americans they serve.

For more information about the RHTP, visit: https://www.medicaid.gov/resources-for-states/rural-health.
