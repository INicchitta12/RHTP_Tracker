About the NC ROOTS Advisory Board

The NC ROOTS Region 2 Advisory Board is being established to provide guidance for the NC ROOTS Hub Action Plan for Region 2. The Board consists of twenty-one (21) members and is designed to represent diverse communities, sectors, and lived experiences across the region. Advisory Board members play a vital role in supporting equitable, community-driven outcomes that strengthen the implementation, sustainability, and long term success of NC ROOTS initiatives.

Application Process Now Closed

The application period for the Region 2 NC ROOTS Advisory Board is now closed. We appreciate the strong interest from leaders across all sectors who are committed to strengthening rural communities, improving health equity, and expanding access to whole person care in Region 2.

Thank you to everyone who submitted an application and expressed interest in serving. If additional opportunities arise in the future, we will share them on our website.

If you have any questions, have ideas for driving rural health transformation, or are interested in supporting Region 2, send an email to Region2NCRootsHub@TrilliumNC.org.
