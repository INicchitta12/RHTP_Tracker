WHAT THIS DOCUMENT SUPPORTS (spec 9.2, Part A of reviewer-coding-instructions)

This is ONE document and it supports the coding on its own: it names the
recipient, the amount, the initiative and the counties served for every one
of the 138 grants, and it states them as awarded --
'announced the awarding of 138 grants'. That is §9.2's Yes test met by an
official governor's release naming the recipient.

THE AMOUNTS ARE MIXED PRECISION. 93 are exact to the dollar; 45 are written
as a rounded number of millions ('$6.38 million'). They are stored as
published, flagged amount_precision = ROUNDED_TO_MILLIONS, and the resulting
gap -- the release's own figures sum to $143,745,821 against its headline
'more than $144 million' -- is reported rather than closed. ADECA publishes
the underlying award file; adeca.alabama.gov and alabamarhtp.com were both
unreachable when this was extracted.

14 OF THE 138 ARE SECOND GRANTS carried in a continuation paragraph that
names no recipient of its own. They inherit the preceding recipient and
carry award_sequence = SECOND. Reading only the list items would lose them.
