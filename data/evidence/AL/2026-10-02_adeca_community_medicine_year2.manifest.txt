(Revised 4.10.2026, Table XIV-J1) carries "$5M for 3 years; $7.3M for year 1" for
Community Medicine and neither award release names a Community Medicine award. Both
later ADECA documents say the initiative has no Year 1 funding and begins in Year 2
(2027). The narrative line is also internally inconsistent: its Year 1 figure exceeds
its own 3-year total. The resources page lists ten closed Year 1 NOFOs and none for
Community Medicine.
