WHY IT IS ARCHIVED (session 83): it is the document that decides Alabama's year1_status.
The Governor's 2026-10-01 release says the round-2 grants "round out year one of funding",
and the 2026-08-24 release says ARHTP "includes 11 initiatives". Round 1 funded five and
round 2 funded five more; the ELEVENTH is the Community Medicine Initiative (mobile
wellness and mobile grocery units), whose Table XIV-J1 reads "Estimated required funding:
$5M for 3 years; $7.3M for year 1". Neither release names a Community Medicine award.
The figure is a PLAN line (§0.3) and is never an award; it is cited only as the record
of an initiative with Year 1 money and no published award.
