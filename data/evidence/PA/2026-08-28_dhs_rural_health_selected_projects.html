    PA Rural Health Transformation Plan (RHTP)

    Eligible Projects

    Below is a list of authorized projects making a tangible difference in access to quality health care in rural Pennsylvania communities.

    Total Awarded to Date:

    $43,000,000

    PA's Rural Health Transformation Plan (RHTP) is providing significant investment to strengthen health care access and infrastructure in rural communities. Millions of dollars are being directed to Pennsylvanian hospitals, health care providers, and rural facilities to support building upgrades, essential equipment, medical transportation, and expanded services, including behavioral and maternal health care.

    PA's rural health projects focus on:

    Technology and Infrastructure

    Including the use of consumer-facing applications that support easy access to primary and specialty care

    Workforce

    Including supporting incentives like upfront scholarships, mentoring, short-term housing, and stipends based on a five-year commitment to service in rural communities

    Maternal Health Services

    Provide comprehensive care management and navigation between prenatal and postpartum care, behavioral health, and other services to support safe and healthy pregnancies and parenting

    Behavioral Health Services

    Like expanding 988 services and continuing public education on crisis response services

    Aging and Access

    Facilitate safe transitions from hospitalization to care at home and support quality of care in rural long-term care facilities

    EMS and Transportation

    Modernize rural EMS infrastructure to improve efficiency and sustainability

    List of Eligible Projects

    The eligible projects were shaped by local needs based on extensive input from rural communities, health systems, community leaders, and state agencies. RHTP focuses on long-term improvements and emphasizes regional collaboration and accountability.
