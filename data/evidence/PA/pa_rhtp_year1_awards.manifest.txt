WHAT THESE DOCUMENTS SUPPORT (spec 9.2, Part A of reviewer-coding-instructions)

NEITHER DOCUMENT SUPPORTS THE CODING ALONE, and this is the same shape as
Georgia's AHEAD roster (session 10). The program page read by itself is an
ELIGIBILITY list, and §0.3 forbids coding eligibility as receipt. The
announcement read by itself names no recipient. Together the announcement
says sixty-six projects were AUTHORIZED to RECEIVE the money and links to
this page as the list of them. Every row cites both.

THESE ARE AUTHORIZATIONS, NOT DISBURSEMENTS. The announcement states
'Distribution is pending approval of selected projects.' Rows therefore
carry validation_source_type = NOTICE_OF_INTENT_TO_AWARD and
disbursement_status = PENDING_APPROVAL. Both intent-to-award and award are
primary under §8; the distinction belongs in the row, not flattened away.

This tranche is the FIRST of Pennsylvania's $193,294,053.98 Year 1 award.
$42.2M of $193.3M is 21.8%; the remainder is not yet awarded and no residual
may be inferred from it (§0.3).
